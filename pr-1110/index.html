Keyboard and mouse WASD (or the arrow keys) fly · the mouse aims (the ring at the cursor glows pink within a 💌's reach, and a blue arc fills round it while the dodge recharges) · on the ground, left click (or hold 1) throws 💌 invites at the cursor: every one that lands joins a ring orbiting the creature, the gaps the hits still needed; a full ring turns to ❤️s and wins it over, its sigil dropping as a rune at its feet: stand by it and press E to leash it; left alone, the envelopes fall out of orbit one by one (enraged ones and legends block them) · right click dodges: a 15 m blink the way she's going, two in hand (pressed a moment early, it goes as soon as it can) · Space rises to the treetops or lands · E on the ground puts down / picks up a sigil (knocked out, she drops her hat: the 🎩 pointer leads back to it, and E on it puts it back on, before any sigil under it) · Q cycles the stack (the bottom sigil, the next put down, to the top) · R her broom's spell, if it has one (Speed: a boost; Ley jump: to the ley line's pulse; Ground pound: up or down at once) · T turns auto-talk on or off (off, she talks while Shift is held) · 2–4 empty slots for now · the mouse wheel, Z/X or +/− zoom · Esc pauses everything (a still for screenshots, with a stamp of where you are; while paused . steps a frame, Shift+. ten) · L saves the playtest log · ~ debug · N next wave · P pause waves · I invite nearest (debug) · [ ] fight scale, ; ' fight speed, , . fight momentum (debug: in the ~ panel too)
The treehouse fly over its balcony at the treetops and she lands on it · walk off its open rim to fly again · E at the map in the middle of the round room opens the sea chart, to sail to another island · the rooms off it: walking up to the mirror, the wardrobe, the cloak pegs, the shoe rack or the bags opens dressing up at just those things · in the Workshop the bay fits her broom's parts (a whole set is her broom's spell), the lathe colours it and the pegboard shows what she's found · E on the hats' floor picks a hat up or puts hers down (hats with spells are found in the forest) · E at the bestiary's book opens the zoo · hold E at the bed to sleep and end the night · time runs at a tenth up there, and nothing reaches her
Gamepad left stick (or the d-pad) flies · right stick aims · right trigger throws 💌s · left trigger dodges · cross rises or descends · square puts down or picks up (held: on the treehouse's bed) · triangle puts her hat down or picks it up · circle cycles the stack · pressing the left stick, her broom's spell · the shoulder buttons zoom · Start pauses · Select debug
On screen at the start, dressing up: walk her room with WASD, change her look, and the 🎶 scroll unrolls once the forest has grown: it is the party spell, so click it (or Enter) to begin · once home's speakers are up, the 🎶 ring at the edge points along the ley line to the next wave, its circle filling as the wave comes · the clock at the top is the time played
Phone drag on the left to fly · the round button rises or descends · the 💌 button throws invites the way you're going · sigil puts down or picks up (on the ground) or cycles (in the treetops) · spell and dash · three-finger tap for debug
