## What I see

- **In the stack:** today it's barely a gold squiggle above her hat. At +50% the flask shape reads, and at +100% it reads at a glance, as big as her head and hat together. +100% is my pick here; the stack's other sigils keep their size.
- **Put down at its stone:** today it reads on the ground; at +50% it's clearly a flask, and at +100% it's a large gold glyph at the legend's feet. +50% is enough here. From the treetops it barely shows at any size: it's dim, and the legend and her party stand over it.
- **Lying in the world:** on the ground the rune sits in her own warm light, partly behind trunks, so the gold washes out. The glow grows at each size, but the flask shape never reads. From the treetops under the canopy it's hidden at every size (by design: only its glints show through gaps).
- **So, for "lying", size isn't what's missing.** It wants contrast: a cooler or brighter gold than her light, or a dark rim like the effects' "dark" variant.
