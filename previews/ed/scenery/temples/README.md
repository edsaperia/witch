# Temple ruins: more variations

These are Ed's asks from 2026-10-11 ("both, but also we should have more variations of these kinds"; "They can also be smaller"), built in the PR "Classical ruins from a seed".

The classical ruins now come from a seed. There are six large plans and four small ones, each with 3 seeded variants. Each variant gets its own collapse (how much has fallen), its own overgrowth (ivy, bushes, a young tree) and its own column counts and sizes.

## `large.png`: the featured pool
These sit on about 80% of islands:
- **greek-temple** and **classical-ruins**, as before.
- **temple-tholos**: a round temple with a ring of columns on a round stepped base and the stub of its inner wall.
- **temple-colonnade**: a long stoa with two rows of columns before a back wall, and stretches of architrave.
- **temple-podium**: a temple on a high stepped podium, with steps up its front, four porch columns and the cella's walls broken down.
- **temple-pediment**: a great pediment fallen flat among the stumps of its columns, with an architrave block tipped beside it.
- **temple-portico**: a portico half buried in a grassy mound, with only the tops of its columns and its pediment showing.
- **temple-theatre**: an overgrown theatre, with tiers of seats in a half ring round a stage.

Each large one has a few fallen pieces round it (drums, a pediment corner, a statue), chosen by its seed.

## `small.png`: scattered in many more area types
- **ruin-columns**: a few columns in a row.
- **ruin-lone**: a lone column, its capital fallen at its foot.
- **ruin-doorway**: a doorway frame standing alone.
- **ruin-altar**: an altar on its steps.

Each small one has rubble and a fallen drum beside it.

## How they draw
None of them is split into a top half that hides like the canopy (Ed: "none of these need their top halves to be hidden"). This applies to every classical piece and the Greek temple too.

## How often they appear
Averaged over 200 islands of 10 areas:
- **Featured pool:** 0.98 an island. The featured roll gives 80% of islands one. The ordinary scene roll can also pick one of these in the area types they suit, so a few islands get two.
- **Distinct featured scenes:** all 20 appeared.
- **Small ruins:** 0.46 an island.
