# The Studio's instruments: a mock (2026-10-23)

Ed (2026-10-23): "The studio has a drumkit (top left), modular synth setup (top right), microphone (middle, spotlit). They open menus when you go near that let you 'play them'. Eventually maybe you can access the music lab from here. I think the record collection is actually next to the DJ booth."

Nothing here is built. The shots come from the game, with placeholder boxes; Alpha would draw the real furniture.

- `1-studio.png`: the Studio with the drum kit (top left), the modular synth (top right) and the microphone (middle, behind her). The spotlight isn't mocked yet.
- `2-record-shelf-by-decks.png`: the record shelf, moved out of the Studio to the east end of her decks on the balcony.
- `3-drum-kit-menu.png`, `4-modular-synth-menu.png`, `5-microphone-menu.png`: what slides in as she walks up to each, in the station menus' style. No E needed.
- `drum-kit.mp3`, `modular-synth.mp3`, `microphone.mp3`: 8 bars of the night's music at half volume, as if heard in the Studio, with her playing over it from bar 2. Everything is played by the game's own engine:
  - **Drum kit:** the night's own drum sounds (kick, snare, hats, a tom fill, claps, open hats). She hits slightly early or late, and each hit snaps to the party's next sixteenth.
  - **Modular synth:** one of the Music Studio's patches, an arpeggio in the night's key, with the cutoff and resonance turned up as it plays.
  - **Microphone:** the game's singer (the alto, "laa"), long notes stepping in the night's key, through her hall.

How playing would work (proposed): the menu slides in when she's near. Keys 1–8 (or clicks) play: drum pads, or the eight notes of the night's scale, so she can never play a wrong note. The arrow keys move through the menu, and she can still walk away.

One catch: in the treehouse, time runs at a tenth and the music slows like a tape. To play in time, the Studio would need the party's music at full speed (quieter, as if from outside), or its own beat.
