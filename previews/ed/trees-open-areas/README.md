# Trees in the open areas: in game, before and after

Ed picked trees for all twelve open areas (mocks in `docs/art-review/mocks/trees-for-open-areas/` on the PR). These are the built game, seed 123, at the same spot in each area: on the ground, then from the treetops (a tree's crown shows only from the treetops; on the ground you see its trunk).

- `moor-ingame.png`: a few lone hawthorns and rowans among the moss and standing stones (subtle by design: 2 to 4 trees an area).
- `stone-shrine-ingame.png`: old yews round the stones; from above, dark yew crowns among them.
- `ravine-ingame.png`: Scots pines and rowans leaning out among the rock spires.
