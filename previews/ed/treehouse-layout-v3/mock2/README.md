# Treehouse layout v3: mock 2, organic (for Ed's OK; nothing in the game has changed)

Ed's answers to the first mock: the corridors are the Ent's branches and the rooms are round; make some real art. He then added: redraw the Terrace too, and give the rooms walls and roofs that are tree canopies, hidden when she's near. This is the game drawing it from its own camera (mock build, branch `claude/treehouse-v3`, `?rooms=labels`).

- `whole.png`: the whole house, every room under its canopy (she's in the Parlour, so its canopy is open).
- `near-terrace.png`: the Terrace, redrawn as one round platform with the balcony as its front lobe. A carved ring marks its middle, the lit decks' desk stands at the front edge, and a rail with lanterns runs round the back. The front is open air: she steps off there. Behind it are the Studio and the Kitchen, the Workshop to the west and the Atrium to the north-east.
- `near-bedroom.png`, `room-bedroom.png`: the bedroom wing; `room-bedroom.png` is at the game's indoor zoom with her inside, so the bedroom's canopy is gone. The back half is walled in upright boards with lit windows; the front half has a low rail so you can see in. The Crow's nest is off its right side.
- `plan-top-down.png`: the plan from above (north up).
- `house.json`: the mock's plan: round rooms (`r`) and curved branches (`pts`, `w`).

Rooms: the bedrooms are 16 m across; the other rooms 13 m; the hubs 9 m (the Atrium and the Boudoir) and 11 m (the Parlour). Branches are 3.2 m wide and 2.5–4.5 m long between rooms. Walking from the Terrace to the bedroom door is about 50 m, about 10 s at 5 m/s.
