# Treehouse furniture: the mock (PR #1017)

The first four pieces, drawn as art in place of their boxes: the gothic canopy bed, the clothes rail (wardrobe), the bulb-lit vanity (dressing table) and the crystal ball on its clawed stand. The other pieces are still boxes.

1. Side by side: the boxes now, the art at house.json's footprints, and the art at 1.6 times (`?furnitureScale=1.6`).
2. The bedroom's back corner, zoomed in, at 1.6 times.
3. The four pieces as a contact sheet, each art pixel 4 screen pixels, at 1.6 times. It uses plain lighting; the game's lighting is in the other shots.
4. to 7. Full frames: the boxes, the art, the art at 1.6 times, and the art by the wardrobe and dressing table.

Made with `tools/house/furniture-shots.cjs` and `tools/house/furniture-sheet.mjs`.
