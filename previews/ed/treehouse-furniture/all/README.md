# Treehouse furniture: every piece (PR #1017)

Ed's answers on the mock: about twice the size, the canopy over the bed's head half, dim rooms with the glows lighting what's near them, and fewer, chunkier pieces of clutter. Every object in the house is now drawn as art in Golf's treehouse v3 rooms.

- `0-every-piece.png`: all 25 objects facing the camera, as a contact sheet with plain lighting. It includes the curtains both open and shut.
- `room-<room>.png`: each room in the game, standing a little south of its middle, zoomed out a step.

Made with `tools/house/furniture-sheet.mjs` and `tools/house/furniture-shots.cjs`.
