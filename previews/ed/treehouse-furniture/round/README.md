# Treehouse furniture for round rooms (PR #1017)

Ed: "furniture goes in round rooms so no need to make everything rectangular as if to go against a wall".

- The pieces along the walls are built straight, then bent round the room's wall: the clothes rail, the vanity, the window, the cloak pegs, the shoe rack, the bags, the desk, the broom stand, the pegboard, the record shelf and the skulls' cabinet.
- The tea, card and map tables are round and stand out in the room, as do the cauldron and the crystal ball's table. The bed stands out into the room, its head to the wall.
