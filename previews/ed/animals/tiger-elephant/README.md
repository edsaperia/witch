# Tiger and elephant (mocks for Ed)

New animals for later islands, each with its own new jungle area type (Ed, via the coordinator, 2026-10-12).

- `tiger-mock.png`: the tiger (replacing unknown-11: jungle, phase 3, bearing 269) and its area, the **bamboo jungle**.
  - The creature: cub, young, adult and legend, beside the lynx and the bear for size. A low, long, heavy-shouldered striped cat. The adult grows a ruff of cheek fur. The legend's stripes burn with spirit-fire, with a smouldering mane and flames along its spine, long pale claws and lit eyes.
  - The area: a bamboo-litter floor, ferns and long grass, dense bamboo groves under tall kapoks, and a jungle shrine in the clearing: a stone tiger on a plinth between two cold stone lanterns, roots over it and offerings at its foot.
- `elephant-mock.png`: the elephant (replacing unknown-28: jungle, phase 4, bearing 265) and its area, the **temple jungle**.
  - The creature: calf, young, adult and legend, beside the elk and the bear for size. The biggest creature in the game, grey, with a trunk, fan ears and tusks. The calf has a short trunk and big ears; the young grows its first tusks; the adult is a great tusker. The legend is a walking temple: old stones, moss and vines on its back, long tusks burning at the tips, lit eyes.
  - The area: red earth, ferns and tumbled stones, huge strangler figs and kapoks with date palms and bamboo, mud wallows, and temple steps in the clearing, a fig rooted on top and two stone elephants flanking the stair.
- Both sheets end with the animals at night, as the game lights them. The elephant reads khaki under the sheet's studio light but blue-grey at night.

Size: the elephant's adult is about one and a half times the bear's adult and well over the art check's 2.1 times the witch (the elk's limit is 2.5). That is a DECISION FOR ED: keep it this big, or bring it down to the elk's size.
