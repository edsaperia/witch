# New animals' legends, redone to Ed's brief (docs/ART-GUIDE.md §0c)

Each sheet shows baby, young, adult and legend, with the witch for scale. Every legend is the elder of its kind, with no generic crystals, flames, wings or ribbons.
- `roe.png` (roe deer):
  - an ancient buck grown tall and grey-muzzled;
  - heavy many-tined antlers (an old buck's "perruque");
  - a pale winter ruff at its throat and moss on its flanks from lying out for ages;
  - bead offerings bound round its legs.
- `redpanda.png` (red panda):
  - an old mountain sage, big and round, its muzzle and whiskers gone white;
  - a long pale ruff like a beard and long climbing claws;
  - prayer beads round its legs.
  - The ribbons are gone.
- `cat.png` (wildcat):
  - the meadow's old queen, big and broad;
  - a lion's ruff of pale cheek fur, long claws and beads round its legs;
  - her crown (it will be gold in the build, from the genome's own magic hue).
  - The ribbons are gone.
- `unicorn.png` (unicorn):
  - the first unicorn, ancient and great;
  - its horn long and ivory, worn and carved with the rings of its age, with no light;
  - a goat's beard, a long dark mane and a grey muzzle;
  - feathered hooves and the bead garlands of those it healed.
