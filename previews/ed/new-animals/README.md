# New animals: mock sheets for Ed (not built yet)

Each animal sheet shows baby, young, adult and legend, with the witch for scale. Each area sheet shows the floor, the small and big pieces, the set piece and the creature.

- **Roe deer** (`roe-deer.png`): a small reddish deer with a white rump, a dark nose, a dappled fawn and short three-tined antlers; its legend's antlers glow.
  - Its area is the **mossy dell** (`roe-mossy-dell.png`): oak, birch and rowan over deep moss and ferns, with an old ring of stones in a glade.
  - It goes on the phase-3 island to the north-west (replacing the "?" island unknown-13).
- **Red panda** (`red-panda.png`): rusty red with a white mask, dark legs and a thick ringed tail (a new tail type); its legend trails ribbons of light.
  - Its area is the **misty terraces** (`red-panda-misty-terraces.png`): fir, rowan and birch on stepped mossy ledges, with a cairn of flat stones.
  - It goes on the phase-3 island to the east-north-east (replacing unknown-03).
- **Wildcat** (`cat.png`): a grey-brown tabby with thin wavy stripes (a new coat marking), an upright ringed tail, big ears, whiskers and white socks; its legend wears a crown and ribbons of light.
  - It replaces the glowworm and lives in the **meadow** (`cat-meadow.png`; that sheet still shows the hare, today's meadow creature).
