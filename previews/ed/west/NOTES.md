# West: jungle (concepts)

These are concept sheets only, and nothing here is in the game. They are made the same way as the south's (see `../south/NOTES.md` for what each sheet shows).

## Jaguar (area: Jungle)
- **Feel:** a heavy, spotted big cat, the jungle's apex. It is powerful rather than fast. Its legend grows glowing crystal spines along its back (the `mane` feature). A black jaguar is an obvious palette variant.
- **How it fights:** a pouncer. It stalks, crouches, then leaps a long way in one bound (the rig's crouch-and-leap) for a heavy hit and a knockdown, then rests. The counter is to dodge the leap.
- **Sigil:** a round, cat-like face with round ears, rosette cheeks and whiskers, in magenta neon. It risks reading as a bear: the rosettes need to be stronger.
- **Jungle:** a floor of leaf litter and seedlings, buttress-root walls, ferns and shrubs, and dense trees. Vine trees (`vinewood`), tree ferns (`palm`) and giant cedars, with sycamore filling in. Set piece: a ring of carved stones round a jungle altar (`ring`).

## Tapir (area: River wallow)
- **Feel:** a gentle, odd, pig-like browser with a short trunk, dark with a pale saddle. Its babies are striped and spotted. Its legend is a walking jungle, trees and flowers on its back.
- **How it fights:** it is mostly passive. When hit it bulldozes, a short heavy charge through the crowd with knockback. Herds stampede together, and the legend's charge flattens a lane.
- **Sigil:** a side-on head with its drooping trunk and two legs, in mint neon.
- **River wallow:** trampled red-mud floor, a slow brown river at the edge, reeds, tree ferns and leaning sycamore and willow. Set piece: a dugout canoe sunk in the wallow (`punt`).
- **Caveat:** the saddle marking barely shows on the dark coat yet.

## Dart frog (area: Cloud forest)
- **Feel:** tiny and jewel-bright (cyan with black spots, and other neon coats as palette variants), the most colourful thing in the forest. Its legend wears a crown.
- **How it fights:** it is fragile but toxic. Touching it or being near it poisons over time (a lingering cloud), and it hops away. Crowds of them are a hazard field rather than fighters. The legend spits poison globs (the web-glob effect, recoloured).
- **Sigil:** a frog seen from above, with splayed toes and spotted back, in cyan neon.
- **Cloud forest:** a floor of deep wet moss, fungi, ferns and bright flowers, mossy vine trees with glowcaps as the fantasy minority. Set piece: a ring of mossy stumps round a rain pool (`ring`).
