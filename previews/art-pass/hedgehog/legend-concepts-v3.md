# hedgehog — v3 readable silhouette concepts

**Concepts.** A — domed quill field and torn ear. B — heavier body, long snout and three bound quills.

**Pick.** A. The quill dome and low heavy body read as an ancient hedgehog without a scene.

**Body-plan requirements.** dorsal spine field, ear/tuft assembly, snout curve, bead sockets, torn-ear decal.
