# dormouse — v3 readable silhouette concepts

**Concepts.** A — oversized round ears and beaded tail. B — heavy cheeks, grey muzzle and brow band.

**Pick.** A. The ears and compact round body carry the dormouse identity at 64–128px.

**Body-plan requirements.** large ear sockets, cheek volume, tail taper, bead socket, muzzle-weathering decal.
