# flyingsquirrel — v3 readable silhouette concepts

**Concepts.** A — folded broad patagium and collar. B — larger membrane edge, tail and eye mark.

**Pick.** A. The folded membrane creates a clear flying-squirrel outline without a body scene.

**Body-plan requirements.** triangular membrane sockets, membrane edge curve, tail cross-section, collar, eye decal.
