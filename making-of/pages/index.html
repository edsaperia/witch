The Making of Coven Rush

You are a witch with a pair of decks in a treehouse. Cast the party spell, and the party spreads out across a forest island, area by area, wave by wave, while you fly ahead inviting its creatures, calming its sleeping legends and keeping the soundsystems standing. This site is the story of how it was made.

The opening, in Ed's words

Ed names the first minutes of a run in nine phases. They are the spine of the game, and of this site.

  1. BedroomDress your witch.
  2. The CastRead the party spell from the scroll.
  3. At the DecksStanding behind the decks in the treehouse.
  4. Step-offYour first step away from the decks.
  5. First SpeakerThe first runestone in the ring turns into a speaker.
  6. The BootThe home speakers power up one by one, the music building with them.
  7. First CountdownThe wave pointer appears and the ley line's pulse sets off.
  8. Wave OneThe party spreads to the first new area.
  9. The Party's OverEvery soundsystem down: the forest falls asleep.
