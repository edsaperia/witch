# Creature size ranks

Ed's 2026-10-30 size rule ranks real animals by broad body-mass cohorts **within each age**. These are ordinal art-direction classes, not a claim that every member has the same measured mass. Nearby classes may overlap and native pixels may tie. No ordering between different ages of different species is promised. An asterisk marks a mythical or extinct design placement.

|Class|Species, smallest cohort first|
|---:|---|
|0|woodlouse, snail, moth, dragonfly, beetle, scorpion|
|1|newt, salamander, dormouse, toad, crab, tarantula (spider)|
|2|mole, stoat, squirrel, flying squirrel|
|3|hedgehog, meerkat, fennec, marten, owl, raven, heron, rattlesnake, fruit bat|
|4|sand cat, arctic fox, hare, armadillo|
|5|fox, badger, otter, raccoon, red panda|
|6|lynx, beaver, roe deer, penguin, jungle python (snake)|
|7|wolf, cheetah, snow leopard, black jaguar, ibex, ram, boar, Komodo dragon|
|8|tiger, lion, stag, sphinx*, griffin*|
|9|bear, polar bear, elk, crocodile, unicorn*, feathered serpent*|
|10|rhino, frost troll*, hydra*, frost wyrm*|
|11|elephant, stegosaurus*|

The explicit strict comparisons are tiger > sand cat, wolf > fox > stoat, and elephant > rhino. All real or design classes separated by at least two steps must also have strictly ordered occupied heights at the same ordinary age. Every species must grow strictly through its four ages; a legend's full visible silhouette must reach at least 1.75 times its adult's.

Reference anchors for these broad placements: Washington's [wolf identification](https://wdfw.wa.gov/species-habitats/at-risk/species-recovery/gray-wolf/identification) and Minnesota's [red fox profile](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/mammals/redfox.html) support the wolf/fox separation. Animal Diversity Web's [sand cat](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Felis_margarita/) and [tiger](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Panthera_tigris/) accounts anchor the cat distinction. SANParks' [elephant comparison](https://www.sanparks.org/conservation/parks/kruger/letaba-elephant-hall/learn-more/african-vs-asian-elephants) anchors the largest living cohort. San Diego Zoo's [tarantula profile](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/tarantula) and Louisville Zoo's [fruit bat profile](https://louisvillezoo.org/animalsandplants/bat-rodrigues-fruit/) keep those species from being treated as tiny generic spiders or microbats. These references do not constitute an exact mass dataset for all sixty-four species.

The numeric audit publishes every pixel tie and measures both game facings and every gait frame. Ranks, visual likeness and natural anatomy still need canonical art review against the combined twenty-redesign roster; this table does not grant release approval.
