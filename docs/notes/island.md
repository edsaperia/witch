- **Island growth / island shape**: how an island's areas are chosen (`src/rules/islandGrowth.ts`, `growIsland`; Ed, 2026-10-11, the journey's irregular islands): from home outward over the areas' neighbours, each step the frontier area nearest home for the island's **reach** that way (`reachOf`: a seeded noise round home, **lobes**; **arms** reaching out, peninsulas; **bays** cut in; each area's own **jitter**), home's own neighbours always first; by its **phase** (the tuning's `map.shapes[phase]`, 0 home near-round, 1 gently lobed, 2 bays and a peninsula, 3 arms and inlets, 4 and 5 strongly irregular; a kind's island its roster's phase, otherwise `map.phase`, `?phase=0..5`). Its land is then every area within the buffer's depth (`map.buffer` × an area × 0.85, about 180 m) of the playable ones, so everything for play stands 150 m or more from the sea whatever the shape. `node tools/islands/map.mjs [--phases] [--seeds] [--out]` draws islands by phase from above. Their real outlines for the sea chart and the dreams: `islandOutline(island, tuning, step)` (`src/rules/islandShape.ts`; the island from `islandOf(kind, world)` or `homeIsland(world)`): the coast's loops in metres round home's middle (`sea` the sea's coast, else a lake's shore), its box, `far` (its furthest coast), the areas' size; exact (the map's own growth and land, `islandLand` in rules/map.ts, sampled every `step` m, 16 by default), so about 0.1 s for a phase 1 island to 2 s for a phase 5 one: remembered once made; for a whole chart, worth making off the main thread or as islands come into view. `islandCoast` (the radii the chart and the dreams draw today) is still the old seeded wobble until they move to it.
- **Land map**: where the island's land is (`src/rules/land.ts`, `makeLand`; Ed, 2026-10-11, the journey's irregular islands and lakes, docs/backlog/journey.md): made once in `generateMap` from which areas are land (the playable ones and every area within the buffer's depth of them: Island growth), sampled every 4 m (first every 32 m, finer only where that changes), smoothed (slivers under about 16 m and crumbs under a quarter of an area's square taken as what's round them), its coast traced as closed loops round the areas' borders (land on the left; a loop round land is the sea's coast, one round water a lake's shore), and each sample's signed distance to the coast (+ on land). It answers "how far from the water?" (`distAt`, `roughDistAt`), "which way in?" (`inward`), "the nearest coast" (`shore`) and "along the coast" (`along`) for her flight's edge and its soft hold (`map.bounds.land`; `keepIn`, `isInside`, `softEdge`, `exitPoint` in rules/mapShape.ts), the beach (its sand by distance from the coast, its width by its own noise along each loop), the hills' easing, sea life, the coast camera (her bearing the coast's way out from her) and the waves' sound; the ground shader reads the same numbers as a half-float texture (`uLand`: distance, sand width, the way to the coast). About 40 to 120 ms to make on the home island. `coastFacing` finds a straight stretch of coast facing a given way (tests and tools; `window.witch.coastFacing`). Not a hex grid: a square one (Ed, 2026-10-11). Lakes have their own map of the same kind (Lakes, below).
- **Archipelago**: where every island lies on the sea chart, how far its shape is turned and the patch it grows in (`src/rules/archipelago.ts`; Ed, 2026-10-12, the journey's tessellation: "the rings can be very relaxed, a pleasing layout is much more important", "you can make the sea larger so that they fit", "keep arms and irregularity" and coasts that mirror each other "like jigsaw pieces pulled apart"; then v3: "more open sea", "the phase rings should hardly show", a minimum gap between islands, islands turned to fit, "you can have larger gaps", and "add back the arms towards the edges of the map, since there's nothing to fit with there"): made once a world (`worldOf(world)` in rules/rosters.ts, about 1.2 to 1.4 s, remembered). A packing of the islands' **foreseen coasts**: each island's own shape (its phase's lobes, arms and bays, `reachOf` with its seed) scaled to its areas (home at its full 64, so it grows into its place as islands are found), plus its buffer ring (`COAST_PAD` by phase, measured against the real coasts). Home in the middle; every island starts at its kind's bearing, as far out as its phase's share of the disc strayed by its seed (`MINGLE` 1.2 of the gap between phase rings either way, so phases mingle); then, round after round, any two foreseen coasts nearer than their sea (`GAP` 1.3 to 3.2 straits, wider where a slow seeded noise opens the sea) are pushed apart, each island pulled a little back toward its bearing and its own distance, its body (`CORE`: its coast trimmed to 1.15 times its median reach) kept a strait in from the bodies' rim and its arms from the disc's (the last `ARM_RING`, 1 km, left to the outer islands' arms out to sea), and every 30 rounds turned (`Island.turn`, 16 tries) to the turn that fits its neighbours best, its arms into their bays (an outer island's longest arm out to sea). Foreseen land fills `FILL` (0.42) of the bodies' disc, the rest open sea. Each island's **patch** (`Island.patch`, metres along 96 angles from its home; `patchAt`) is the sea nearer its foreseen coast than any other's, the strait's midline. `generateMap` grows each island in its own shape turned by its turn (`reachOf(..., turn)`, `growIsland(..., turn)`), its playable areas kept half a strait, the buffer's depth and most of an area inside its patch, and its land map clipped half a `STRAIT` (400 m) in from the patch's edge by true distance, so no two coasts ever come nearer than a strait (an arm grown past its forecast cut along the strait). The disc's radius is `CHART_METRES` (14 km), one true scale for every island and world (`metres`). `islandAt` (app/chartLayout.ts) returns the archipelago's places; `ringAt` the old rings. `node tools/islands/chart.mjs [--worlds 0,1,2] [--outlines]` draws today's rings beside the archipelago (outlines: about a minute a world, with each world's nearest coasts measured and any under a strait marked red).
- **Lakes**: areas of water inside an island (`src/rules/lakes.ts`; Ed, 2026-10-11, the journey's irregular islands and lakes): from phase 1, `map.shapes[phase].lakes` (fewest, most) of them, each `lakeSize` areas (neighbouring ones merge into one water), never home or its neighbours, the rest of the island its size and still joined (`placeLakes` in `rules/islandGrowth.ts`: grown with the island, then each lake from the area most hemmed in by the island's own); ringed by land as deep as the buffer's, so never on the coast. Their water is the lakes' own land map (`map.bounds.lakes`, its "land" the water: `lakeDepth` + on a lake), not the island's, whose coast stays the sea's alone (her flight's edge, the beach, the sea's life). `map.isLake(cx, cy)`; not playable, not the buffer's; no trees, paths or anything placed on the water or within `lakes.shore` m of it. No beaches but a small shore (Ed, 2026-10-11): the area's own floor running down into the water, wet and darker at the edge, a broken pale line lapping there, shallows where the floor shows under the water, deepening to a lake blue lighter than the sea's, the night sky in it (the ground shader, `uLakes`); each lake lies in a hollow (`terrainAt` in `rules/terrain.ts`, so the drawn hills, the trees' lie of the land and the chart's heights all have it): its water one flat level (`lakeLevel`: the lowest ground round its shore, 1.2 m under; lakes within 48 m of each other share one), the ground rising from it no steeper than 0.6 of the hills' slope limit (a bowl carved out on high ground, blended into the hills: `LAKE_SLOPE`, `LAKE_REACH`), never under the water round it. Some have **koi** (`koiPonds`, `lakes.koi` of them by the run's seed: 2 to 5 orange, white and gold koi circling its deepest water, drawn in the shader, each as long as a baby creature is drawn tall (Ed, 2026-10-12: "large, similar to the size of a baby creature": `koiScale` in render/ground.ts, about 4.2 m)). She flies over them in the treetops but never lands on the water (coming down over it she holds low, lift 0.3, and lands once ashore); on foot she stops `lakes.pad` m from the water, sliding along it as she steers, and so does every creature (`creaturesAshore`, each step, nudged along the shore so none stands stuck); one thrown at a lake stops at its shore (her knock too). Creatures fighting or marching on something across a lake head round it the shorter way along its shore (`roundLakes`, combat's `round`), travellers' routes keep off it (`planRoute`). The sea chart's outlines carry their shores (`islandOutline`, `sea: false`). `node tools/lakes/shots.cjs [dist] [seed] [phase] [out]` shoots one in play and checks the shore stop and the landing; `node tools/lakes/leylines.mjs` counts the ley line's links over a lake (about 10 to 15% of them, straight: DECISION FOR ED whether it should go round); `node tools/islands/map.mjs` draws them from above. **Biome lakes** (Ed, 2026-10-12 on the sea chart; in play since 2026-10-23): each lake takes the look its island's place gives it, one source for the chart and the game (`src/rules/lakeLooks.ts`: `islandBiome` by its bearing and phase, `lakesOf` each lake by its shore loop, seeded): a **glacier** in the north (a faceted 3D mesh of ridged ice peaks and broken seracs, nothing flat, its peaks kept `lakes.glacierClear` m under her treetop flight: `glacierMesh` in `src/render/lakeLooks.ts`), **lava** in the east, an **oasis** in the south, a **sinkhole** in the west (a 26 m shaft drawn down past its rim in the ground shader), and on the diagonals a **glacial tarn** crowded with ice, a **tar pit**, a **hot spring** (the rust pool: Grand Prismatic's rings, travertine terraces on its shore and steam, `lakes.steam`, `steamVents`) and a **lily pond**; drawn by the ground shader from the lakes' map and a map of their looks (`uLakeLooks`); koi only in plain water; looks only, they play as water. Their **shore dressing** (Ed, 2026-10-23: `src/rules/lakeShore.ts` lays it out along each lake's shore loops, seeded, off paths, clearings and the gameplay's ground; `art/lakeShore.js` draws it; `src/render/lakeShore.ts` one batch round her): palms and grass by an oasis, bones and drowned trunks in tar, reeds round a lily pond, ice chunks by a tarn or a glacier, glowing lava rocks and scorched trees by lava, travertine crust by a hot spring; and a lava lake lights the trees round it (`lavaGlows`: the nearest two, a campfire's light). `?lakelook=<look>` draws every lake in one look; `node tools/lakes/looks.cjs` shoots them (a look, or `kind:look@x,z` on its island) and `node tools/lakes/looks-map.mjs` lists each island's.
