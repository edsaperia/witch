# legends config history and detailed field notes

Moved from `config/legends.json` on 2026-10-29 for #1459. Original notes below are preserved verbatim, including quotes and dates. They record the design when written; the config and rules remain the source of current behaviour.

## Overview

Legends, redesigned (Ed, 2026-10-05; issue #87). What they are for (Ed, 2026-10-06): 'The idea of legends is that they provide a different layer of tasks to the tasks to the game, that are mostly optional. If you are quick and can do the quests you can get buffs from them; if you are lucky and find a relic they can be very powerful allies; and if you are losing, their babies get hurt during sieges and they get enraged (ideally this helps the loss spiral happen faster and more dramatically). I think you should be able to get the buffs at any time the legend is sleeping, not just before the soundsystem is made.' The rules: Asleep by default (soundsystems no longer wake them), dreaming of a creature (the quest): bring that creature's sigil into its area while its quest is open (as long as it sleeps, its area's soundsystem on or not: Ed, 2026-10-06; done before the area's wave, the area is friendly too and the ley line moves on) and you get its buff; it sleeps on. Restless while its area has none of its own kind (any state: wild, happy, leashed ones parked there, babies), its dream a nightmare (restlessness 0 to 1 over angryAfter seconds), calming back to sleep as soon as one of its kind is there again; at 1 it's angry. Each legend looks for its kind every check seconds (Ed, 2026-10-06: "the legends could check for own species in area once every five seconds without issue"), each on its own beat (staggered by its id, so they never all look at once). An angry one calms too, back to sleep, as soon as one of its kind is back in its area (Ed, 2026-10-06: "Angry legends should go back to sleep once one of their own species is back in their area"; restlessness 0, a buff once earned kept). Going back to sleep (calmed, or worn down) away from where it lay, it walks home first at homeSpeed m/s and lies down where it spawned (Ed, 2026-10-06: "When legends go back to sleep, they should go back to their circle first and sleep in the spot where they spawned initially"). Happy once a relic is put down within placeRadius metres of it while it sleeps (or is restless): you get its buff, and it defends. Buffs, once earned, are never taken away. relics (Ed, 2026-10-06): giant half-buried party objects, one per perAreas areas the waves reach, at least one (Ed, 2026-10-11: today six whatever the size), each of its own kind while they last: twelve kinds, the six bottles and their mirror images (Ed, 2026-10-11: "flip the current kinds so there are now 12 kinds"; a kind ending Flip draws its bottle mirrored), each in each of colours (Ed, 2026-10-11: "We can also recolour today's bottles for more variants. We shouldn't use the same colour reflected on the same map": a glass and its glowing liquid, the i-th plain kind's own colour the i-th; another written <kind>~<colour>), never one bottle in one colour twice on a map, mirrored or not, while they last (36 looks), then round again: one, of a seeded kind, just inside the home area's edge (homeInset metres in from it, out past home's circle, at least clearOfTreehouse metres from the treehouse so it's never hidden behind it); the rest scattered over areas at least minRemoteness from home (each the first of up to candidates seeded areas at least spread metres from those already down, else the farthest), none within minGap metres of another, never in a cleared place (a legend's clearing, a set piece's, a soundsystem's), and its middle at least clearOfCircle metres past a legend's circle (about the biggest bottle's half: Ed, v1628, "Legend and runestone in the same place"); its relic sigil lies on the ground sigilOffset metres south of it (Ed, 2026-10-06; 12 once its bottle was drawn in the world, 2026-10-09, its mound's front reaching about 9 m toward the camera; back to 6 with the bottles at a third of their size, 2026-10-11, the mound's front about 3 m out, and still more than the leash's runeRadius so standing on the bottle doesn't pick it up): stand on that and press the sigil button (E), as for any placed sigil (the leash's pickRadius): it's a relic sigil in your stack. attack (angry and happy alike; Ed 2026-10-05: long range, slow, less damage): a legend never leaves its area (it stands at home) but reaches range metres (about 2 to 3 areas); every interval seconds it winds up for windup seconds (telegraphed), then lobs (a bomb landing after lobFlight seconds, hitting everything within lobRadius) or beams (a line range long, beamWidth wide, burning for beamTime seconds), chosen per species (beam lists them); each volley hits up to targets of them (one lob or beam at each), damage a hit (the witch: one point, as ever); enraged animals within wornReach metres go for a happy one (it wears down); with nothing in reach it looks again every recheck seconds (balance, 2026-10-06: looking every step made 30 awake legends cost 17 times the rules). A happy one heals to whole over healTime seconds when nothing's near. (Balance builder's values, #80: legend damage is the game's most sensitive knob.) Angry: the witch on the ground and her posse (her leashed ones), never happy creatures; with no witch in its reach, its first lob or beam goes at the nearest standing soundsystem within the tuning's legends.bombard.range, for legends.bombard.damage (Ed, 2026-10-06: "Legend bombards, but prioritises you"; rules/combat/legend.ts). Happy: enraged ones only. Worn down (its health gone), a happy one goes back to sleep (its buff kept), an angry one too.

## charge

The long charge (Ed, 2026-10-05: "charging legends do long distance charges knocking away everything in the way... but they'd have to go back to their area after each one"; "medium speed (they're huge, and heavy), and they have a big slowdown arc afterwards. They can curve somewhat as they charge to hit their target"). species: the legends that charge instead of lobbing or beaming (those whose fight move is a charge). Every attack.interval seconds at home it picks its target as ever (attack.range), lowers its head for windup seconds (its first lane shown on the ground, laneShown metres of it), then runs: building speed at accel m/s a second to speed m/s (about her run), turning toward its target at up to turn degrees a second (a sharp sidestep or a blink beats it; drifting doesn't), until it's past its target or has run attack.range metres; then it brakes over brake metres, turning wide at arc degrees a second as it slows. Everything in its lane (laneWidth metres wide) but its own side and its own kind is hit once: damage, creatures knocked aside knockback metres (less as it slows); the witch loses her one point and is thrown aside and staggered as by any ram (tuning witch.knock: rules/knock.ts). It runs straight through scenery. Then it walks home at returnSpeed m/s to where it lay (rest seconds there before it may charge again): the trip home is its real cooldown. closeMoves: false (Ed, 2026-10-05: "stick with the long range one for now"): legends use only their long-range attack, near or far; true brings back their close-up move sets (slam, nova, charge; spin in phase 2) for wild legends.
