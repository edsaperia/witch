## Overview

Creature movement in a fight (Stage 5; DESIGN.md 'Creature movement'). A species with a profile moves by the weighted sum of its fight behaviours, at speed m/s in a fight (Ed's motion scale pass: about the witch's speed, 12 to 20 m/s; times tuning fight.speed; else combat.fightRun), changing velocity at most accel m/s each second (heavy creatures turn slowly). Lengths (radii, a move's from/to, trigger, height) are at the fight's scale (tuning fight.scale). Behaviours: arrive (to its attack's reach of the target), keepRange (between near and far of its attack's range), orbit (circle the target at radius of its range), strafe (sideways, swapping way every swap seconds), slot (to the place its pack's tactic gives it), separation (away from creatures within radius), cohesion (toward its pack, beyond radius), wander (jitter), dodge (step out of a shot's line within radius), light (drawn to the nearest light within radius, a soundsystem, fluttering round it: the moth). tactics: what a pack of its kind (one kind, one side, one target) picks between, by weight, re-picked every repick seconds: surround (slots spread round the target), pincer (two halves from opposite sides), hitAndRun (in only when its strike is ready, then back out to a ring), volley (a line across at range, firing on the beat), swarm (no slots: rush in, spread and jittery), flank (slots round at the target's back, the way it's heading, striking only from behind: the fox, the marten pack). move: a signature move on a cooldown: charge (with momentum: it lowers its head for windup seconds, builds speed at accel m/s each second up to speed m/s down a locked lane, carries on through what it hits until it's overshoot metres past its target or time seconds have gone, then brakes at brake m/s each second turning in an arc toward its target at turn degrees a second; when the target is from..to metres off; hits on reaching it), ambush (still until the target is within trigger metres, then a burst at speed for time seconds), burrow (from `from` metres off it goes under, untouchable, tunnelling at speed times its run for up to time seconds, and surfaces under its target striking at once), leap (when its attack is ready and the target is from..to metres off, a leap of time seconds and height metres, landing just short of it: its attack, a pulse, lands with it). Every species has one (Ed's species pass, 2026-10-05: all 30). pursuit (optional): its sprint closing in from afar, m/s (else combat.pursuitRun; slow kinds don't sprint). Ed's species pass (2026-10-05) adds to these: a charge's backup (m/s it backs off while it lowers its head: the ram's run-up), pair (its pack mates whose charge is ready set off with it: the stags), curl (rolling curled up, taking curl times the damage: the hedgehog, the woodlouse); a leap's strike (it lands a blow on its target, not a slam round it: the lynx's pounce, low and quick); an ambush's strike (it attacks the moment it springs: the snake); dig (the badger: when its target is within trigger metres it digs in for time seconds, rooted, no knockback, taking armour times the damage, biting without a lunge), block (the beaver: when a shot comes at it within radius, or its target winds up at it, it braces behind its tail for time seconds, rooted, shots taking armour times the damage and blows half, then slaps back at once), trail (the snail: a patch of slime every `every` seconds as it moves, radius metres, drying after time seconds, slowing the other side on it, the witch to slow times her speed), flash (unused since the wildcat took the glow-worm's place: when its target is within radius, a burst of light dazzling the other side within it, slowed for time seconds, the witch to slow times her speed). legends (Stage 5): a wild legend's move set: it works through pattern in a loop (each move a combat.json attack, wound up long and telegraphed), approaching to each move's reach; at phase2.at of its health it roars into phase 2: its phase2 pattern, moving at speed times as fast, its moves cooling down in cooldown times the time. bySpecies: a species' own legend move set, the same shape (Ed's planned direction: each legend its own). bodies (Ed, 2026-10-05: 'animals attempted not to bunch up while moving; the distance they want to keep can depend on their size'): each kind's body radius (m, an adult's), times level's scale (baby, young, adult, legend); the room each keeps is that times space by level (Ed, 2026-10-08: babies, young and adults drawn half again as big, their room grown with them and their bodies (reach, hits) not; a legend keeps its room, already 3.2 times an adult's body, as a wider one pushed its circle's baby awake); two creatures keep (r1 + r2) of those rooms times factor plus margin metres apart, eased apart at up to push m/s (times the fight's speed) whatever they're doing, looking at no more than neighbours of the others nearby (on a grid of cell metres), for creatures within range metres of a witch (about her view on the ground). Swoop (the wild flyers: the owl, the bat, the raven; hotel's phase-3 flyer table, built 2026-10-09): hostile (wild or enraged), it circles its target radius m out at circle m/s, height m up and out of reach (no 💌, blow or talk reaches it; drawn raised over its shadow), wavering by jitter of its radius (a bat's erratic flight); with its attack ready, it telegraphs for windup seconds, rising to rise times its height (the raven climbing, its shadow growing), then dives along a straight line at speed m/s at where its target will be when it gets there (lead of the target's velocity times the dive's flight time, at the moment it lets go: walking straight on gets caught, a blink beats it), overshoot m on past, skims low for bottom seconds and climbs back up over climb seconds; dives such dives a bout (a bat's several), then cooldown seconds circling. Only in the dive and at its bottom can it be hit or invited; its dive touches every foe it passes, once each, knocking her back knockback m. Leashed and party flyers fight as before.
