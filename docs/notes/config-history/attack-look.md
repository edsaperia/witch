## Overview

The attack look (its spells) (Ed, 2026-10-27: "they should glow / flame / splash when they land, and have a variety of effects and colours and looks"; the gallery mock, previews/ed/spell-gallery). A spell is four layers, each from its own place: its delivery and side effect from the rules (config/combat.json, the attack's delivery and modifier), its element from here (bySpecies, else its kind's biome by byBiome, else default; an attack's own body.element tag (config/attacks.json, Hotel's attack grammar) wins over both, its other names read through aliases), its power by its level. The element dresses any delivery: art/attackLook.js bakes each element's parts into one atlas (its core, trail bits, impact bits, ground mark, beam beads and area tiles, in its palette of four, dark to hot, and once in grey for a party animal's neon), and render/leash/attackLook.ts draws them from one pool. elements: palette (dark, mid, bright, hot), accent (a second colour: bloom's leaf), core (orb, shard, droplet, crescent, spark, rock, void, petal), trail (embers, sparkles, drips, wisps, sparks, dust, smoke, petals), impact (how its bits fly: burst, shatter, splash, ring, crackle, rubble, poof, blossom), bits (the bits' shape: flame, shard, droplet, wisp, fork, chunk, puff, petal), mark (what the hit leaves on the ground: scorch, rime, puddle, halo, char, cracks, pool, flowers), beam (its beads along a beam: flame, crystal, drip, ripple, fork, stone, smoke, petal), area (its tiles over a quake's or pulse's ground: the same names as mark), solid (its core and bits painted, not glowing: earth's rock, shadow's dark). effects: the side effect's motif by its modifier, shown by shape, never colour. scale: how many art pixels each of the atlas's pixels is drawn (whole pixels: 2, so its parts read at the play zoom, where an art pixel is a pixel and a half on screen); power: size and particle count by level (1 young, 2 adult, 3 legend). particles: an adult's trail bits a second and impact bits. danger: the shared danger frame every wild spell wears, whatever its element (Ed, 2026-10-27: "composability and telegraphing"; never red, the adult rank colour): white warning dashes (dash and gap art pixels, marching at march pixels a second) between dark edges, filling with the time left, flashing for flash seconds before it lands; the safe circle's dark hatch at hatch opacity. looks: a body's look tag (an acorn, a pinecone, a log, a pebble, a feather, a can, a leaf, a spike, an icicle, a rolling ball) draws its core as that thing instead of its element's, in its own palette or (none given) in its element's colour; its trail and hit stay its element's. aliases: the attack grammar's other element tags (config/species-attacks.json) as one of these. overrides: a species whose look wins over its attack's own tag (the temperate deer in bloom, the hare's stamp a gust), for variety within a biome.
