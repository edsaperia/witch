# Art checks

Part of Witch's notes (CLAUDE.md is the index): the art generator's check, the style bible, the genome compare, the lab and export commands. Moved here from CLAUDE.md as it stood (housekeeping, Ed 2026-10-23: a note an area, so pull requests add to their own area's file, not one shared file); every rule kept. Add to this file, not CLAUDE.md, when you change this area.

The art generator (`art/`, entry `art/generator.js`) and the Witch Art Lab (`tools/art-lab/`) have one more check, run from the repository root before every push that touches them, and in CI since 2026-10-13 as its own workflow, **Art check** (`.github/workflows/art-check.yml`, not required until it has proven steady): on a pull request that touches only creature and area folders it runs just their checks (`--only`, about a minute; `tools/art-check/scope.mjs` decides, `src/artCheckScope.test.ts`), shared art code, the Art Lab, the style file or the check itself get the full run (about 40 minutes on a runner), a change with nothing of the art in it is skipped, and every push to `claude/prototype` gets the full run; its log and the silhouette sheet are the run's `art-check` artifact:

- `node art/check.mjs` — builds the lab, opens the source page and the built page in headless Chromium (no script errors, a bestiary card for every species, the scene drawn; each on a page of its own, drawing while the rest runs), and draws everything the art generators make (every creature at every level, face, texture and silhouette, the trees and bushes, the area types and their ground cover, the witch and the party witches in every pose, the hats, the sigils, the dancefloor and its speakers, the treehouse, the props, scenes, relics, paths and party objects, the attack effects, the sleeping legends, the live rig) and holds each to its rules: sizes by level and against the witch, what may glow, anchors inside the sprite, strips and loops repeating exactly, no flecks, silhouettes apart, every material coloured, and the art lint. What it checks and every number it holds them to are the check's own, not copied here (the docs pass, 2026-10-28: a 12 KB copy of them stood here, its numbers drifting from the code's): `node art/check.mjs --list` names its tasks, each line of its report says what it checked, and each check's comment in `art/check.mjs` says why. The rulings in it: the style's `creatureScale` 1.5 that its sizes against the witch are times (Ed, 2026-10-08); the party relics at a third of their built size (2.3 to 5.4 m across: Ed, 2026-10-11); two tree species growing in one area looking alike from the treetops a warning, not a failure (the coordinator for Ed, 2026-10-14; before that every pair across all the trees had to differ); a species' growth that is only scale a warning (`WARN growth, not scale`) until every species clears it (the menagerie review, docs/art-review/menagerie-review.md). **Its speed** (the coordinator's ask, 2026-10-13: a full run took about 2 h on one browser page and died at the cloud's session limits): the sprite checks are named tasks and the art lint goes a species or an area at a time, dealt out over a pool of pages (`--pages N`, by default the machine's cores less one; each page its own renderer process) so every core draws, the report put back in the tasks' order so the output reads the same; `--only fox,fen` keeps every check that walks the species or the areas to those ids (a builder's own creature and area), `--no-lab` skips the two lab pages, `--tasks a,b` runs only those tasks (`--list` names them), and `--times` prints what each task, lint unit and pass took. A builder's quick run: `node art/check.mjs --only <species>,<area> --no-lab`, about a minute.

**Style bible**: `docs/STYLE.md`, the short rulebook every piece of art follows (the grid and whole-scale drawing, the master palette and area ramps in `docs/style/`, outlines, scale against the witch, the readability ladder, contact sheets); art builders apply it to every batch. **Art guide**: `docs/ART-GUIDE.md`, the living style guide from the art director's reviews (what reads at each size, palette, silhouettes, clumps, how an area carries into its creature and legend, past failures, proposed checks). Builders read it before each art batch.

`node tools/genome/compare.mjs <dir>` compares every creature sprite (all species, levels, frames, facings, plain, in gear and woken, and the sleeping legends) with those a checkout of the old art in `<dir>` draws, pixel for pixel: run it on any change to the genomes or builders that shouldn't change the art (`git worktree add <dir> origin/claude/prototype`).

Related commands: `node tools/art-lab/build.mjs` writes the self-contained lab page to `tools/art-lab/dist/witch-art-lab.html` (git-ignored; published from `dist/` by `npm run build`, `tools/labs/publish.mjs`); `node art/export.mjs [style.json] [out dir]` exports every asset as albedo and normal-map PNGs with `manifest.json` (default `art/out/`, not committed); `node art/preview.mjs animals|trees|wind|treeheights|areas|sets|lights|soundsystems|sigils|witch|home|relics|grounds|decor|country|landmarks|scenes|partyobjects|partypatch|lineup|lake|paths|pathpieces|species|canopy|speakers|ring|disco|discostrip|discolooks|tufts|sway|partywitches|legends|effects|genome|silhouettes|partyrelics|witchgen|faces|textures <list> <png> [scale]` renders lit preview sheets. They need Playwright's Chromium (in cloud sessions, under `/opt/pw-browsers`).
