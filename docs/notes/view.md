# The view

Part of Witch's notes (CLAUDE.md is the index): what the renderer draws over the world: tilt-shift, the ground and its hills, ley lines, the sky and moon, smoke, pops, the debug overlay and test sliders. Moved here from CLAUDE.md as it stood (housekeeping, Ed 2026-10-23: a note an area, so pull requests add to their own area's file, not one shared file); every rule kept. Add to this file, not CLAUDE.md, when you change this area.

- **Pixel snap** (`pixels.snap`, on; Ed, 2026-10-29: "the pixels are just hard to read"; `render/view.ts` resize, `View.cssPixel`): the picture's pixel on the page is the pixel size rounded to the nearest whole number of device pixels (Ed's pick), its css size that over the DPR: at a DPR of 1.25 a pixel size of 3 is 3.2 css pixels, 4 device pixels (it was 3.75, the browser drawing them 3 and 4 wide by turns, uneven strokes crawling as the camera moved); at 1.5, 3.33 and 5; at 1 and 2 nothing changes. The view's about 6% tighter at 1.25 and things 6.7% bigger, the canvas-drawn HUD with them; not through the telescope (its own finer resolution). The camera's sub-pixel glide (`app/shake.ts`) moves the canvas by whole device pixels too, never part of one (a css pixel at 1.25 is 1.25 of them, and the canvas moved by part of one is resampled). `?snap=0` turns it off. `node tools/shot/pixels.cjs` measures it: the drawing buffer's and the screen's runs of one colour and the share of edges with an in-between pixel, at each DPR, tilt-shift on and off, `LOOK=before` as it was.
