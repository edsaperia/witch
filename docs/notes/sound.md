Online beat authority (canonical6546524308, 2026-10-31): `Sound.update` accepts guest-only `{shareBeat:true}` to skip `Music.keepBeat`; host/solo leave it absent and keep their existing audio-stall beat shift. The session forwards the host's shifted beat anchor before the next replay frame. Unit checks prove an unchanged guest beat, unchanged absent-option behavior and host/guest snapshot agreement after a shift. This does not audition a guest stall: exact-head canonical browser/audio evidence must still establish what is heard during resync. No platform/audio or potion mixing code is changed by this option.

Hue Spin (recipe 108, PR1541) reuses the shared trip filter from PR1536. Its cutoff and flutter follow the same wake-relative cycle as the screen colours; default cutoff sweeps two octaves above/below the shared centre frequency. `soundDepth` changes the range, and the existing sound mix controls the dry/wet envelope. No additional nodes or sound sources. Pause holds the phase, replacement selects the new treatment, expiry/Stop restores dry sound. Night/Heat Vision remain excluded from the fallback.
**Night Vision and Heat Vision audio** (`app/sound.ts`, `platform/audio/potionAudio.ts`, 2026-10-31): Night Vision plays one original rising electronic whine on entry, then silences ordinary effects and music while preserving creature sounds at their normal configured volume. The creature route includes speech, replies, howls, enraged/refusal growls, legends' calls and charging/hooves, roars, animal dancers' footsteps, snores and sea-creature splashes/groans/pours. Synthetic happy-state pops, spell/projectile impacts, UI, witch speech, music and environmental ambience remain ordinary audio. Both SFX routes share the existing master volume, limiter, voice-budget object, active-voice list and rate-limit map; separate reverb returns prevent ordinary ambience or spell tails leaking into the creature route.

Heat Vision silences music but leaves ordinary effects audible. Its entire creature bus, including already-sounding voices, noise, vocal formants and reverb, passes through two complementary windowed delay taps adapted from the singer shimmer technique. At full strength the delay slope produces a 0.5 pitch ratio; during the final two game-clock minutes the ratio rises as `2^-strength`. This is a granular delay transposition, with a 3–63 ms delay window and possible grain texture, not a source-only note change. Zero strength restores the exact dry route and releases all four control-buffer sources and both delay taps. An independent one-source, filtered-noise mask breath follows the game clock and the lab's breath volume/rate, fading with the effect. Breathing and the startup whine bypass pitch and ordinary-audio isolation while retaining the shared SFX master and limiter.

All gates derive from the current potion each frame: full strength at wake, ordinary audio returning over the final two game-clock minutes, immediate baseline on stop/replacement. User volume settings are never overwritten. New nights reset the routes, and watchdog replacements inherit the current music/SFX gates and creature pitch; deliberate music silence is not a watchdog fault. Unit tests cover routing, live control automation, source disposal, fade/replacement and one-shot startup identity. **Pending coordinator audio checks:** `node tools/sfx/check.mjs`, `node tools/sfx/mix.mjs`, and `node tools/sfx/run.mjs` require an allocated browser/offline-audio slot; the coordinator owns these slow checks under CLAUDE.md's current builder rules. No listening or offline spectral validation is claimed by the unit tests.
