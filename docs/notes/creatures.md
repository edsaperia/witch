# Creatures' art

Creature sizing (Ed, 2026-10-30; coordinator 6520479974 and 6520542543) uses the visible standing witch's **43 occupied pixels**. This supersedes the earlier game-wide projected-fit instruction: witch, house, props and other non-creature art retain the old renderer path. Only creatures opt into camera-projected support. Ellipsoids use oriented radii, rounded boxes their support, round cones endpoint spheres, and masked planes their clipping parallelogram. Ray acceleration and its 96-step budget remain unchanged.

`art/genome/size-ranks.js` assigns [broad body-mass classes](projected-fit-data/ranks.md) within each age. With class rank r from 0 to 1, baby targets are 0.75+0.2r witch heights through r=0.9, then 0.93+2.7(r-0.9); youth targets are 1.1+0.4r; adults 1.8+1.2r. Legends target at least 1.75 times the same species' rounded adult height. Targets measure the full occupied silhouette, including decoration. Nearby classes may tie after pixel rounding; tiger>cat, wolf>fox>stoat, elephant>rhino and class gaps of two or more must remain strict. Mythical and extinct placements are design choices, not measured living-animal masses.

Projected support supplies the initial scale; a bounded native raster search fits occupied pixels, with a small vertical sampling-phase search where rounding skips a height. Final rendering always starts from restored model data. A bounded 128-entry cache retains only scale and phase. Generation cost is included in the [combined numeric audit](projected-fit-data/age-sizes.md); resource gates are not relaxed. `node tools/body-graph/age-size-audit.mjs <output-directory>` measures all 1,040 gait/facing cells, palette indexing and species pages. Its eight-largest-species scenario is an estimate, not measured runtime residency; rigs, gear and sleep/wake sets are excluded. The earlier [projected-only table](projected-fit-data/sizes.md) is historical and superseded, not evidence for the combined size model.

The mole and quadruped `fit:"current"` fields remain valid, but final age sizing governs their output too. No gameplay radius or reach changes accompany the visual sizes. Snow leopard alone uses bounded, separated decorative-mote placement to preserve its attachment count; other species retain their previous sampler.

Ed's combined-release decision (6520690688) enables exactly twenty roster redesigns by default: fennec, dragonfly, wolf, dormouse, fruit bat, woodlouse, frost troll, hydra, crocodile, crab, meerkat, flying squirrel, marten, raccoon, badger, stoat, otter, black jaguar, bear and arctic fox. Explicit `id:current` still selects the original genome and a separate cache identity. Lion, raven, frost wyrm, polar bear, unicorn, mole, snow leopard and beetle remain Current. Contact sheets must label redesigned/current and compare the combined art at matching game size and facings. Cloud art checks and canonical visual review remain release gates.

Part of Witch's notes (CLAUDE.md is the index): genomes and templates, textures and palettes, evolution kits, faces, the live rig, the sleeping legends' art. Moved here from CLAUDE.md as it stood (housekeeping, Ed 2026-10-23: a note an area, so pull requests add to their own area's file, not one shared file); every rule kept. Add to this file, not CLAUDE.md, when you change this area.

- **Sleeping legend**: an area's legend of its species as it lies asleep for centuries (`art/legends.js`, `legendForm(id, st, { frame, facing })`): half sunk in its area's earth, head down, eyes shut, its glow gone to stone and old wood, mossed and grown over so it passes for a boulder, log or mound, in 2 slow breathing frames at the legend's scale, `origin` the ground under its middle. No waking sequence or awake redraw yet (Ed, 2026-10-04: "just the sleeping form"). Batch 1: bat, marten, elk, stoat, owl, snail; batch 2: wolf, fox, badger, boar, stag, hare, bear; batch 3: lynx, otter, beaver, ram, squirrel, dormouse, salamander; batch 4: toad, raven, mole, hedgehog, woodlouse; batch 5: snake, moth, spider, stag beetle; the wildcat (in the glow-worm's place, 2026-10-13) (`LEGEND_POSES`): all 30; every species since has its own (its folder's `legend.js`: 64 today).
- **Asleep / nap**: a creature lying asleep (`c.asleep`, set by the rules when the party's over; Ed, 2026-10-06): the view settles it down and draws its nap (`art/naps.js`, `critter(id, level, frame, st, "towards", { nap: true })`, baked as the "nap" set, each level in 2 breaths): eyes shut, lying, curled, tucked, coiled or flat by species (`NAP_POSES`). Not a sleeping legend (sunk and mossed, its own `legendState`).
- **Genome**: a species as data (#79; since 2026-10-13 one file a species, `art/creatures/<id>/genome.js`, assembled by `art/creatures/index.js`, which `node tools/creatures.mjs index` writes sorted by id, and re-exported as `GENOMES` by `art/genome/species.js`; Ed, 2026-10-13: every animal PR edited the one file and merges mangled neighbouring records. **A new creature** is a new folder with its `genome.js` (`export const GENOME_<ID> = { id, name, template, builder, ... }`, a named export: the Art Lab's build inlines every module into one page), its `sigil.js` (`SIGIL_<ID> = { neon, strokes }`, the strokes from `art/sigils/strokes.js`'s building blocks; `art/sigils.js` assembles `SIGILS` and `SIGIL_NEON` from them), its `legend.js` (`LEGEND_<ID>`, the sleeping form `art/legends.js` documents) its `nap.js` (`NAP_<ID>`, `art/naps.js`) and its `creature.js` (`CREATURE_<ID>`: the config files' per-species entries, section by section: `movement`, `radius` and `legendMoves` (`config/movement.json`'s), `attacks`, `strength`, `ranged`, `kite`, `temperament` and `traits` (`config/combat.json`'s), `voice` and `character`, and `pack` (`config/packs.json`'s kinds: its pack combat); the readers (`rules/creatureConfig.ts`) assemble the tables the rules always had, the schema is `config/schema/creature.schema.json`; `node tools/creatures.mjs show <id>` prints everything about one species), and a run of the tool. Still in their tables: its island in its area's `art/areas/<id>/kind.json` (its line of the kinds table: it takes a placeholder's slot, and that placeholder's `config/kinds/<id>.json` goes) and the tuning file's `army.heavy.species` and `population.swarm.classes` lists (Ed's knobs); `node tools/creatures.mjs list | check | adopt` list them, check the index and the records (CI, `src/creatures.test.ts`), and move any record a merge pastes back into `species.js`'s array): its template, builder, palette, proportions (`body`, `head`), parts (`parts`: ears, tail, feet, horns, antlers, tusks) and markings (`coat`); `speciesOf` makes the builders' species object from it, `genomeHash` its bake-cache key. Edit the record, not the builder, to change a species.
- **Template**: a body plan (`art/genome/templates.js`): quadruped, avian, flyer, serpent, insectoid, squat; its builders, its sockets and the part tags each allows, tags that exclude each other, its gait (for the live rig) and, for the quadruped, its **size curves** (four numbers, baby to legend, for each proportion that changes with level).
- **Material mask / palette row**: a creature sprite baked once as which material each pixel is (and whether it's an outline), coloured at draw time from a palette row (`art/genome/palette.js`: `bakeMask`, `paletteRow`, `paintPixels`, `PALETTE_GLSL`); a **palette variant** is a curated coat (`PALETTE_VARIANTS`: frost, ember, shadow, gold, moss, rose, ash) that recolours a species with no rebake.
- **Surface texture**: a creature's fur, feathers, scales, plates, shell or bristles (`art/genome/texture.js`; Ed, 2026-10-05, #119): its coat's pixels gathered into small cells shaped by its kind (strokes running back and down the body, scallops, a diamond lattice, segments, bands round a whorl, long spines), each sharing one normal so the game's stepped light breaks along tufts rather than smooth contours; three or four chosen tones by a fixed light from above (the lifted top `M.BODYL`, the body, its shade, a core shadow), cell by cell; dark seams at a cell's lower or trailing edge; and lumps on its volumes. A baby's coarse and soft, a legend's fine and rich. The genome's `texture` (`kind`, `size`, `stretch`, `flatten`, `contrast`, `seam`, `lumps`, `tones`) over its template's; the rig's pieces wear it too. `?texture=0` (the style's `texture` knob) draws creatures as before. Under the art director's stylised bake (`?style=bold|ref`, `style.artStyle`) the stamps only shape the normals (and seams, by `texDetail`), and the bake lays the tones; `texDetail` (0 cel shapes to 1 fine strokes) and `hueShift` are the style's knobs.
- **Evolution kit**: a species' genome `levels` (four records, baby to legend, any of them null): each level's own proportions (its `body`, `head`, `coat` and `parts` over the species' own: `humpK`, `front`, `neckAng`, `tuskSize`, `tuskCurl`, `bands`...) and its own `features` (`GENOME_FEATURE_KINDS`: `mane`, tapered curved spikes along the back or belly; `wisps`, flames off its tusks, horns or spine; `eyeglint`, its eyes shown past a hump; `stones`, standing stones on its back with moonlit runes; `claws`, long claws off its feet; `moss`, moss and heather on its flanks; `tails`, brush tails fanned along its rump (a kitsune's seven, fox-fire tips; `replace` for its own tail); `ruff`, locks round its neck; `brambles`, thorny vines with berries (the genome palette's `flower`) over its body; and the head and coat knobs `mask` and `beads`; the twisted horn, `horn3d`, is a part: `parts.horns: "twist"` with `head.horn`, a ram's curl by `ease`, `tighten` and `thorns`), drawn by `evolve3d` (`art/creatures3d.js`) from the model's landmarks, each feature on its rig piece (Ed, 2026-10-05: "the evolved design per stage, not just being larger"). It replaces a legend's single `legend` feature where a species has it. The boar is the pilot; the badger (the moor's walking stones) the snail (a tower the forest built; its builder reads `body.shell`) and the fox (a kitsune) the ram (bronze thorned horns, brambles) the woodlouse (its builder reads `body.plates`: a fortress with moonstone spires) the wolf (spirit-fire over a spiky mane; `frost` on its muzzle) and the owl (its builder reads `down`, `tufts`, `discRim`, `bars`, `cloak`, `eyesRing`) follow. A species with a legend level no longer also grows its old `legend` feature.
- **Body profile**: a quadruped's girth along its length, its width and depth set apart (Ed, 2026-10-13: "a key issue with a lot of animals is that they end up sausage shaped; we need them to be able to have a thicker chest and a slimmer abdomen; this needs separate width and depth in the body"; `body.profile` in `art/genome/species.js`, read by `quad3d`): stations from the front back, `shoulder`, `ribcage`, `waist`, `loin`, `hip`, each `{ w, d }` (or one number for both), `w` its width side to side and `d` its depth back to belly as multiples of the body as built there (the back line kept, the belly lifting as `d` falls; a station left out is 1). With no profile the body draws exactly as before (the three loaves); with one the torso is lofted through the stations, each slice the loaves' own envelope there scaled, the forelegs standing as wide as the shoulder and the hind legs as the hip. The big cats, the canids and the deer have theirs. `TOP=1 node art/preview.mjs lineup <ids> <png>` draws each row again from straight above (`PROFILE=0` the bodies with no profile, `LEVELS=2,3` those levels only), for a before/after sheet.
- **Spine**: a quadruped's back line as a shape of its own (Ed, 2026-10-13: "giving animals the possibility of having a distinct spine along its back, which can be anything from a tall crest to a recessed depression"; `body.spine`, read by `quad3d`): a height in model units at each of the body profile's stations (or one number for the whole back), `+` a crest standing along the back line (a boar's bristles, a stag's shoulder line), `0` flat (as built), `-` a groove cut down into the back between its muscles (a big cat's, a lean dog's); straight lines between the stations, fading out at the neck and the rump; the back line is the body's own at each place (the loaves, the withers, the croup), so a crest rides the withers and a groove runs into the loin, and the ridge's spikes (`coat.ridge`) stand on the crest. `PROFILE=0` on the lineup preview draws the bodies with neither profile nor spine.
- **Body knobs from Ed's review** (2026-10-13, the review sheets; `quad3d`, each 1 or off as built): `body.tailK` and `body.tailL` (the tail's thickness and length, scaled from its root whatever its kind: the snow leopard's thick tail, a young one's short one, a marten's bushier with age), `body.pawK` (its feet's size), `body.sprawl` (how far out a sprawler's elbows and stifles go, with `stance: "sprawl"`: the crocodile's), `head.skullY` and `head.skullZ` (the head flatter or wider) and `head.jawLine` (a flat jaw's lower jaw as wide as the upper with the mouth a dark line between them). The polar bear's (levels 0 to 2 only), unicorn's, badger's, rhino's, crocodile's and snow leopard's genomes carry the review's shapes; [`review-bodies.jpg`](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/archive/art-review/mocks/review-bodies.jpg) (on claude/ed-shots since the docs pass) is their before and after.
- **Expression**: a creature's face (`art/genome/expressions.js`, `EXPRESSIONS`): neutral, angry (brows down to the middle over narrowed eyes; with the woken look when enraged), happy (eyes closed up in smiling arcs; party animals and friendly areas' creatures) and dazed (crossed-out or odd eyes, worried brows; while stunned), drawn by the generator as part of the face from the head and eye anchors, sized in pixels so the smallest read (`gear.face`, `faceUp`; brows in `M.BROW`, ink on a light coat, pale on a dark one). Each template has a **face style** (`face`: brow bar, tuft or ridge; happy arc or squint; dazed x or wobble) a genome can override. Neutral draws exactly as before; from behind the face isn't drawn. The view picks it with `expression(c, time)` (`src/render/looks.ts`), and the live rig swaps in the head piece baked with that face (`rigParts(...).faces`).
- **Open jaw** (Ed, 2026-10-13: bites, roars and yawns): a snouted four-legged animal's jaw (`art/creatures3d.js` quad3d, `gear.jaw` 0 shut to 1 wide) turns about a hinge at the back of its cheek, a third of it lifting the muzzle and the rest dropping the lower jaw as a hard edge of its own; open air between the jaws, only the throat, the roof and floor's insides (`M.MOUTH`, a warm red-brown; a species' `mouth` colour) and a pink tongue drawn, and teeth by the genome's `head.bite` (`fangs`: its four canines; `flat`, the hoofed ones' default: two small lower incisors; or `croc`: rows of cones), sized by the snout's depth; `head.jawCoat` keeps its lower jaw, muzzle's underside and chin in its coat rather than its pale belly or muzzle (the lion, bear, stag: Ed's animal review). Two faces open it (`JAW_FACES` in `art/genome/expressions.js`, baked like the expressions and as the rig's head pieces, `rigFaces`): **bite** (angry, wide) for the last `BITE_LEAD` seconds of an attack's wind-up, through its lunge and for `ROAR` seconds after a legend wakes or turns, and **yawn** (eyes shut) while a napper stretches awake (`expression` in `src/render/looks.ts`). `node art/preview.mjs jaws <ids> <png>` (HEADS=1 zoomed) and `node tools/rig/strip.mjs <ids>` draw them.
- **Silhouette check**: each species' shape (young and adult, facing us) shrunk to 24 px and compared with every other's by overlap (`art/genome/silhouette.js`). Since Ed's 2026-10-30 ruling, `art/check.mjs` warns only when silhouette, palette, actual sprite size and pattern are all close (`art/genome/distinctness.js`); look-alikes never fail. Real-animal likeness comes first (docs/ART-GUIDE.md section 4); growth-by-level checks are unchanged. The original shape-only check caught the dormouse reading as a squirrel, but shape alone is no longer a reason to change an animal.
- **Live rig**: on by default (Ed, 2026-10-05: "let's put what we have live"; `?rig=0` turns it off to compare; #79 stages 4–5): four-legged creatures and snakes, wild, enraged (their own woken pages: red eyes, the red tint) and party animals (happy or leashed: their gear, collar, hat and glasses, baked on the head piece, a page per gear; shoes aren't, the legs being discs), on the ground when drawn at least `rig.minPx` (40) art pixels high, baked frames in the treetops, except the levels in `rig.alwaysLevels` (legends: rigged at any size), put together each frame from **rig parts** (`art/genome/parts.js` `rigParts`: torso, head and tail pieces baked at five headings, the other three mirrored, with their pivots, and discs to string legs and serpent bodies; a leg's discs lit as slices of an upright limb, not balls, so they read as one limb), baked by the art workers into one atlas page per species and level (`rig` jobs, keyed by the genome's hash, the least used let go past 48), and laid out by `src/render/rig/rig.ts`: legs as two-bone IK chains stepping on a trot's phases with a duty factor by speed, a serpent as a follow-the-leader chain weaving behind its head, the body leaning into acceleration, crouching before a charge or a leap, stretching in a charge, tucking its legs in the air, its tail swinging out on turns. `node tools/rig/strip.mjs wolf,snake` draws frame strips of a scripted run (`SHOES=boots` as a party animal in those shoes); `node tools/rig/demo.cjs` (ARENA=...) screenshots the game with it; `node tools/rig/perf.cjs` times 300 creatures with it off and on.
