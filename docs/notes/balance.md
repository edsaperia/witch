# Balance

Part of Witch's notes (CLAUDE.md is the index): the balance simulators and bots, fighting value, populations and costs, the playtest log. Moved here from CLAUDE.md as it stood (housekeeping, Ed 2026-10-23: a note an area, so pull requests add to their own area's file, not one shared file); every rule kept. Add to this file, not CLAUDE.md, when you change this area.

A whole run, headless, for sanity (after a rules change, or overnight): `node tools/balance/playthrough.mjs --seed 123 --minutes 15` plays the real rules with a simple bot (it 💌s, carries sigils to the next siege, rests to heal) and reports exceptions, NaNs, anything off the map and busy creatures that stop moving (about 5 s a game minute; `--watch <ids>` follows creatures second by second).

The balance simulator (`src/rules/balance.ts`, run by `node tools/balance/sim.mjs`; a quick version in `src/rules/balance.test.ts`): a headless model of the sieges on the real map and wave order, a few seconds a seed. It prints markdown tables, for 60 s and 300 s waves: the idle player's loss, the enemy's fighting value wave by wave, the skill spread (the wave a player growing at 10 to 100 F a minute lasts to) for each pacing variant (the populations alone; with attrition on the march; with the director's reinforcements; both; no merging), and the catch-up check (a player growing at 30, 50 or 70 F a minute, from the start or from a later wave). It grows the areas as the game does (the same `growthLevel`). `--seeds`, `--gaps`, `--skills`, `--cap`, `--growth`, `--starts`, `--fight`, `--attrition`, `--director base,perWave,power`, `--alphas`, `--expected`, `--weights`, `--per-wave`, `--health`, `--home`, `--quick`, `--no-area-legends` (every area's own legend, Ed 2026-10-04, is on by default) try numbers without editing the tuning file. Run it after any change to populations, combat levels or soundsystem health, and post its tables. The creature-state model (issue #87: invite only, happy defenders, a leashed army, enraged sieges on the nearest soundsystem, never their own kind) is `src/rules/states.ts`, run by `node tools/balance/states.mjs` (`--seeds`, `--gap`, `--skills`, `--policies defend,third,leash,babies,relay,mass`, `--kin-fight`, `--cap`, `--health`, `--dazed`, `--relics`, `--relic-every`, `--restless`, `--legend-range`, `--aoe`, `--hazard`, `--legend`, `--approach`, `--leash`, `--berries`; a quick version in `src/rules/states.test.ts`): survival by invite rate for each way of splitting invites, the pool of wild creatures, each area's own fight by how much was invited before, and the siege targets. `node tools/balance/endgame.mjs` (`--seeds`, `--gaps`, `--skills`, `--policies`, `--cap`, `--hurry at,factor`) traces those runs to find the point of no return and how long losing takes after it (Ed, 2026-10-05). `node tools/balance/buffs.mjs` (`--seeds`, `--gap`, `--cap`, `--skills`, `--policies`, `--relics`, `--no-sim`) models 💌 throughput under stacked legend buffs (`src/rules/throughput.ts`: letters a second, the share landing, animals reached at once, and the per-animal hit gap) and runs the state model with 💌s in place of talk times (`letters`). `node tools/balance/fight.mjs` (`--seeds`, `--sizes`, `--mixes`, `--levels`, `--moves`, `--time`, `--radius`, `--set path=value;...`) runs the real rules headless (`newGame`/`stepGame`, unpaused, waves off): the witch on the ground among N wild creatures of a mix, standing or circling them at a share of her ground speed, counting the hits she takes a minute, then the hits she'd take filling that crowd with each 💌 build. `node tools/balance/fightbot.mjs` (`--seeds`, `--areas`, `--waves`, `--builds 0,2,4,7`, `--bots circle20,circle35,circle50,kiter,greedy,skilled`, `--limit`, `--start babies,young,adults`, `--json`, `--report`) plays real fights with 💌s (after #89): a bot inviting a whole wild area at its wave-W population, its kind's own movement, no new rules; hits taken, knockouts and time per fight by wave and buff count (the builds as `invites` tuning, since buffs don't change 💌s in the rules yet). `node tools/balance/waves.mjs` (`--seeds`, `--gap`, `--skills`, `--policies`, `--cap`, `--json`, `--report a.json,b.json`) compares faster waves for the endgame (Ed, 2026-10-05: waves rushing once the run is lost, `rushAt`/`rushFactor`/`rushNow`, or a ramp over the whole run, `rampTo` with `rampBy` or `rampPct`) against the sieges hurrying, runs stopping at a game time (`maxTime`). The balance tools share `tools/balance/lib.mjs` (`openRules()` loads the rules through Vite's SSR; `arg`, `list`, `nums`, `mean`, `median`, `pct`). `node tools/balance/bottrace.mjs` (`--seed`, `--bot`, `--island N`, `--kind <kind's island>`, `--game`, `--feed`, `--time`, `--every`) prints a bot's run as a trace, a line each time what it's doing changes (for reading why a bot plays as it does; runbot's `--game` plays the bot game's own options). `node tools/balance/logistics.mjs` sweeps area size × treetop speed (Ed, 2026-10-05: treetop mode for travel, ground mode for fighting; animals' walk never a knob): the areas the witch can visit a wave, the party animals' walk to the frontier, whether a defence reaches each new soundsystem in time, and how long a player lasts when every fight waits for the witch and her posse (`logistics` in `rules/balance.ts`).

- **Fighting value (F)**: a side's strength by Lanchester's square law, Σ √(hp × dps) (`rules/power.ts`): young 11.6, adult 40, legend 76 (the level gap, 2026-10-07; 15.5 and 29 before), babies 0.
- **Population growth**: every area starts with `population.start` (a baby and a young: Ed, 2026-10-05) and, while wild, gains `population.growth.perWave` creatures a wave (0.5, one every other wave: DECISION FOR ED, 2026-10-06) at levels by `growth.weights` (`rules/growth.ts`): kept as counts (`game.growth.pending`) until a witch comes within the creature simulation radius or the area wakes, then made out of her sight. The debug overlay's `wild` line counts them.
- **Strength**: a species' number (Ed, 2026-10-05: "just a number that goes up and down"), its multiplier on health and damage (`combat.json` `strength.species`, 1 when not listed; `strengthOf` in `rules/combat.ts`), never a legend's; its areas hold 1 / that many (`countScale` in `rules/growth.ts`).
- **Berry cost**: berries to evolve a level (`toEvolve(level, tuning, species)` in `rules/berries.ts`): the strength the level gains (`berries.cost.by`: "power", hp × dps, or "value", its square root) over `berries.cost.per`, rounded, at least 1: by "value" (Ed, 2026-10-05) 3 and 8 for a species of normal strength since the level gap of 2026-10-07 (4 and 4 before: Ed, 2026-10-06: "Double the amount of berries animals need to evolve."; it was 2 and 2), so a berry buys the same fighting value for every species. Where they're eaten (Ed, 2026-10-27): only her leashed ones eat (happy and wild never); following her, a baby eats none (one berry at a sigil she's put down makes it a youth), a youth eats the berries it passes up to one short of adult and leaves the rest (`lastBerry`: adults are strong and heavy, she can't fly with one, so the last berry is only eaten at a sigil she's put down), and a hurt adult eats to heal; a berry also heals a hurt youth, and counts toward its growing either way.
- **Pacing variants**: the balance simulator's ways of keeping pressure on without a cliff (Ed, 2026-10-04): **attrition** (only a share of a won siege's survivors march on) and the **director** (reinforcements for the areas the next wave wakes, by a budget growing with the wave and, by α, with the player's power). Simulator only so far.
- **Power meter**: the debug overlay's two lines: the party's F (leashed + parked, by level, berries and invites) and the enemy's (every marcher, and each siege with its count and its soundsystem's health).
- **Playtest log**: `src/platform/playtestLog.ts`: a sample every 10 s of play on this browser (localStorage `witch.playtest`, the last 8 runs); L or `?playtest=download` saves it as JSON. `?timing=1` (kept for the tab; `?timing=0` stops it; `src/app/timing.ts`) adds the transition's steps to the run as `timing`: the marks from the treehouse's map table to her decks (`chart:open`, `chart:mounted`, `chart:island1`, `chart:islands`, `chart:sail`, `chart:flown`, `chart:go`, then the new page's `witch:*` and `game:frame1`, `game:arrived`), carried across the sail's reload, in wall ms from the first with each step's own time, and the main thread's long tasks (over 50 ms). Each run has its comparability `key` and each sample its `scene` (docs/notes/performance.md **Like with like**): `node tools/playtest/compare.cjs <log> [<log>]` splits a run by scene and compares two only like with like.
- **Balance simulator**: `rules/balance.ts` and `tools/balance/sim.mjs` (see Testing). The full-run bot, `tools/balance/runbot.mjs`, plays the bots of `src/rules/bot.ts` (the bot game's too) through the real rules for the first 30 minutes (skilled, crude, idle, hover; `--set`, `--happy N`, `--feed` to lead her young ones to berry patches, `--guards`/`--keep` for how many she parks at the next soundsystem) and reports the first knockout, knockouts, invites, the share of each woken area invited, angry legends, quests and soundsystems lost. The opening bot, `tools/balance/opening.mjs`, plays the real rules from her first step to wave 1, recruiting the nearest areas three ways, and counts the legends it angers.
- **Bot / bot game**: `src/rules/bot.ts`, the balance builder's bots (skilled, crude, idle, hover) as one pure module: `newBot(kind, options).decide(game)` gives back the controls a player would, step by step, and `doing` says what she's up to in a few words. `tools/balance/runbot.mjs` plays it headless; the game's **bot game** (the start screen's 🤖 Bot game, or `?bot=skilled|crude`) plays it in real time: a seeded witch (no character creation), the bot casts the party spell itself, the player's input doesn't steer her (the zoom and `~` still work), a corner tag (`ui/botGame.ts`) with what she's doing, 1×/2×/4× (keys 1, 2, 4) and Esc back to the start screen. Tactics changes go in `bot.ts`, so they show in both.
