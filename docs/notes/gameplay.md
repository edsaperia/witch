# Creatures in play

Part of Witch's notes (CLAUDE.md is the index): inviting, the leash and sigils, combat and sieges, creature states, the legends and their quests. Moved here from CLAUDE.md as it stood (housekeeping, Ed 2026-10-23: a note an area, so pull requests add to their own area's file, not one shared file); every rule kept. Add to this file, not CLAUDE.md, when you change this area.

- **Talk / invite**: inviting is by 💌s (issue #87; `invites`, `src/rules/invites.ts`): on the ground she throws them at the cursor (left click or 1), one at a time; each that lands fills the creature's meter (`invites.hits`, by level), and full, a wild one is happy (healed to full) and its sigil drops as a rune at its feet, which she picks up (E) to leash it. Wild legends can't be invited. The old proximity talk (on the ground she talked by herself, no button: Ed, v244; emoji bubbles taking turns for its talk time, babies 3 s, young 6 s, adults 12 s; `invite.talkRange`, `invite.cancelDistance`, `invite.talkTime`) runs only with `invites.on: false`.
- **Babies on her leash** (Ed, 2026-10-11; docs/backlog/gameplay.md "Babies"; `src/rules/babies.ts`): following her on the ground, her babies walk in single file along where she's been (her `leash.trail`), in stack order from the bottom, `leash.babies.gap` apart; while she's in a fight (a wild creature within `combat.defendRadius` after her or hers) they scatter `leash.babies.scatter` (55 m) to a quarter more off to the side away from it (the way fixed when it starts: `leash.scatter`), off the screen (Ed, 2026-10-26: "they should scatter further, e.g. outside of my camera view": at zoom 3 on his 774×448 canvas the ground in view reaches 31 m to each side of her, 24.5 m below and 49.6 m above, so 55 m clears every edge), kept on the island's land (pulled back toward her till it is: `LeashControls.land`, 6 m in from the coast) and pulling on her not at all while scattered (`loadOf`'s `scattering`); Ed: "no areas are dangerous for babies", so where they run doesn't matter otherwise; one more than `leash.babies.farAt` from her moves at `leash.babies.farSpeed` (travelling too). No leash is drawn for a baby. A baby eats only at its placed sigil, a berry within `berries.babyReach` of it (the berries she'd feed it glow brightly while a baby's sigil is next to go down), and `berries.babyCost` (1) berry makes it a youth. Its weight ignores its species' strength: `leash.weight.levels[0]` in the treetops, `leash.weight.babyGround` on the ground (8 fill either allowance), its leash's tension measured from its place in the line.
- **Followers in the treehouse** (Ed, 2026-10-24: "when she's in the treehouse, her followers should switch to idling in the home area"; "they should also defend it, as if their sigils are placed in the home area"; `src/rules/houseGuard.ts`): while a witch is perched (up in the treehouse, on its floor, not knocked out), the creatures on her stack (babies too; a party legend stays put as ever) are seen by the rules as placed at spots of their own in the home area (`houseGuards`, `leashView`): they travel there, rest by their spot and guard the whole home area as placed-sigil creatures do, sieges included; a baby there doesn't eat berries, as it would at a real sigil (Ed, 2026-10-24). The spots (`guardSpots`, once a map) are spread over the home area, clear of the dancefloor's speakers, the treehouse's foot, the area's border and the coast, one a creature by id. No sigil is put down and her stack and leash are unchanged; off the treehouse they're hers again and follow her. Per witch, by whether that witch is perched.
- **Sigil stack**: the sigils of the creatures leashed to the witch, floating above her head, newest at the bottom. The Sigil button (E; gamepad X; touch "sigil") puts the bottom one down as a rune on the ground (a **leash point**) or picks a placed one back up. A carried relic's sigil (the gold flask) is one of the stack (Ed's playtest, 2026-10-08: "sit in the stack, and cycle when it cycles"; `stackOrder`, `cycleStack`, `leash.relicAt` in rules/leash.ts): picked up, it goes on at the bottom, cycles with the rest, and the bottom sigil is what E puts down (Ed, 2026-10-08: "if the flask is in the bottom slot, it's what you put down when you press e"): a flask, given to a sleeping legend in its clearing, else on the ground where she stands (the relic `dropped`: its sigil there, picked up again like any sigil); a creature's, as ever (in a clearing too, for its quest).
- **Party animal**: an invited creature, in party gear, dancing on the beat (`beat.bpm`). Some wear **little party shoes** (Ed, 2026-10-05; `SHOE_STYLES`, `shoe3d` in `art/creatures3d.js`): trainers, glitter, platforms, heels, boots or light-up trainers (soles in its neon), seeded, fitted to a hoof, a paw or a claw; on the live rig a shoe piece on each foot steps and lifts with the leg. Wild and enraged ones go barefoot.
- **Combat**: `rules/combat.ts` (the fight's step and the sieges; split into `rules/combat/`: `data.ts` the attacks, levels, strength and types, `targeting.ts` who fights whom and finding a target, `hits.ts` a hit landing and what a knockdown does, `legend.ts` the legends' moves; all re-exported from `combat.ts`), its attacks as data in `config/combat.json` (levels' health and power budget; melee, shot or quake; windup, cooldown, modifier). Party (leashed) against wild; same kind never fights same kind; wild young and up attack the witch on the ground.
- **Siege**: a woken area's wild creatures marching on its new soundsystem (`combat.sounds`, health `combat.soundsystemHealth`; home is "home", the dancefloor). A fallen one is **ruined** (its party over, no wave wakes it again); its besiegers march on to the next-nearest. The run is **over** (`game.over`) when none stands. When a soundsystem comes, its area's wild babies turn happy and dance; when it falls, its happy babies run off home for good (leashed ones stay hers), and its besiegers, marching on, no longer count as their home area's kin (Ed, 2026-10-06).
- **Wild watch / chase**: coming down in a wild area, its animals stare at her for `wildWatch.time` (the warning, its red rising) before they attack; she can walk or rise out in time and nobody follows (`rules/wildWatch.ts`). Once they attack, they chase her out past their edge as far as `combat.leaveArea`, and a 💌 of hers landing on one provokes it (`wildWatch.provoked` seconds) to come out after her too, so sniping or kiting from just outside the edge doesn't work (Ed, 2026-10-13; the pause page's "#770 Chase" sliders).
- **Enraged**: a wild creature a wave has set on a soundsystem (`siege`, `enraged`): angry red eyes (the art's `woken` look), and it can't be invited.
- **Swoop**: a wild flyer's attack (the owl, the bat, the raven; `movement.json` `swoop`, `stepSwoop` in `rules/movement.ts`): hostile, it circles her out of reach, drawn raised over its shadow (`flightHeight`; `aloft`: no 💌, blow or talk reaches it), telegraphs (a red ring tightening round its shadow), dives along a line at where she'll be (`lead` at release; its aim locked `dodge.a.commit` before it drops, as a strike's is, so a blink then gets her clear), skims low (`bottom`) and climbs back; open to hits and 💌s only in the dive and at the bottom. Hers fight on the ground as before. `node tools/smoke/swoop.cjs <dir> owl|bat|raven` films one.
- **Area legend / sleeping legend**: the one legend of its kind in about a fifth of the areas (`legends.share` 0.2: Ed, 2026-10-08; half before; never home), asleep (sunk and mossed over, scenery) near the top of its circle; waves don't wake it: it grows restless, then angry, while its area has none of its kind, and goes back to sleep when one is back or when worn down; a relic by it makes it happy (DESIGN.md, "Legends, redesigned"); in its circle it reads at a glance (Ed's round 14 playtest): the sleeping form's outline in its sigil's neon and a light floor (`uLegend` in `render/sprites.ts`), a soft neon glow on the ground under it asleep or happy (`legendSeen` in `render/leash.ts`), knobs `wildLegends.seen`; asleep it is its nap (`seen.nap`: art/naps.js, the animal itself lying asleep) rather than its mound (art/legends.js), and every wild legend draws at `wildLegends.scale` 1 (the scene's pixels: its breath a squash in whole pixels) with its light in `seen.steps` steps (round 15).
- **Legend circle explainer**: the panel beside a legend's clearing while she stands in it on the ground (Ed, 2026-10-06: "text appears on the screen to the side of the circle explaining mechanics to do with legends"; `src/rules/legendCircle.ts`: `legendCircleNear` (any state), `circleLines`, `circleShown` (its fade); drawn by `drawCirclePanel` in `src/render/leash.ts`, `.legend-panel` in `index.html`): Ed's four lines with the dreamt sigil (at its level) and a relic sigil as icons; its boon named (Ed, 2026-10-06; `boonWords`) until Ed's 2026-10-18 change, since when it names none (see **Legend buff**); restless, angry and happy say so; beside the circle on screen, the other side if it would run off; `node tools/smoke/legend-panel.cjs` shoots it.
- **Happy legend**: an area legend at peace (by its quest: rules/quest.ts; or the debug key O); since 2026-10-18 it gives no buff (see **Legend buff**). Home has no legend (Ed, 2026-10-05).

The pack is the seed's roll (`rollPack`, rules/fightArena.ts: one time in eight a lone legend, else one kind or, one time in three, two, 2 to 6 of each, young or adult, at most 8; kinds with an attack at every level, `FIGHTERS`) unless `?pack=` names one. Its panel (ui/fightArena.ts, down the right edge): the seed, **🎲 Random pack** (the next seed's roll), the pack typed in, **↺ Fight again** (J), **🔗 Link**, the fight's speed (1×, ½×, ¼×, ❚❚: `fightSpeed`, main.ts steps the game by it), and the attack each kind fights with today (config/combat.json, read only). - **Wild watch**: coming down into a wild area (not home, partified or friendly) for the first time in a while, its young and adults stop what they're doing, turn to look at her and back off to `wildWatch.hangBack` m, for `wildWatch.time` seconds (5.5: Ed, 2026-10-07, "slower ... time to run away"), then attack (`src/rules/wildWatch.ts`, `c.watchUntil`); nappers stretch awake first. Leaving or rising before it's up calls it off (they settle, don't chase; the next visit starts over). Once they've attacked it's once a visit: `game.wildEntry` remembers the area until she's been away `wildWatch.forget` seconds (20); a second witch landing doesn't start it over. The rig turns their whole bodies to her (`RigLook.lookAt`). **Rising aggro**: over the watch each watcher reddens with a pulsing red outline (`render/aggro.ts`, in the instance's glow) the whole area together (the screen edges' red that went with it is gone: Ed, 2026-10-07), scaled by its **danger** (its watchers' fighting value over `wildWatch.dangerF`); `aggroOf(g)` gives it to the view and the music.
- **Dream / quest**: a sleeping legend's thought bubble (Ed, 2026-10-08: a pixel cloud like the speech bubbles, little puffs rising to it from the sleeper, `render/thoughtCloud.ts`, on the 3 px grid; one symbol at a time by turns, its face, then the sigil, now and then the flask's relic sigil instead: `dreams.cycle`), the sigil (in its level's variant) of the creature it wants; put that sigil down in its circle while its quest is open (as long as it sleeps) and it wakes to fight for her (it gave its buff till Ed's 2026-10-18 change; see **Legend buff**); done before its wave, the area is friendly too (rules/quest.ts); its bubble points the way (Ed, 2026-10-06) to the runestone of the nearest area, to her, whose creature it dreams of, partified or not: a pixel arrow (`render/compass.ts`, the eight compass points, north up the screen) and the way in words under its cloud (`dreamWay` in rules/dream.ts: "north-east, 420 m"), worked out again every 15 m she moves.
- **Legend clearing**: the small circle each sleeping legend lies in, near its top (Ed, 2026-10-06; `map.legendClearings`, sized by `legendClearing` in the tuning), where nothing grows: a ring of trodden earth and pale stones round it, brightening while she stands inside; quest sigils and relics count only inside it (put down outside, the circle flashes: the `outsideCircle` leash event). Its **floor**: weathered flagstones with the legend's sigil carved in, mostly overgrown (`legendClearing.floor`, drawn in `render/ground.ts`, the carving's mask from `render/legendCarving.ts`). A wild baby of the legend's kind lives in it, keeping to it (`Creature.circle`, `keepsToCircle`) unless leashed, and dancing there once its area's party comes. Its **rim kit** (art builder 3's #262, `layout.rim`): small pieces (stones, cairns, boulders, toadstools, stumps, posts, none over a metre) just past its floor, all the way round (`rimNear` in `rules/forest.ts`, `legendClearing.rim`). Its **grove** (Ed, 2026-10-06: "an area of the tallest trees around each legend circle", blending "back smoothly into the rest of the forest"): round it the forest swells, thicker and more of the area's two tallest kinds, drawn bigger, strongest at its edge and easing out over 30 m, each tree jittered (`legendGrove` in `rules/forest.ts`, `legendClearing.grove`). Both go all the way round (Ed, 2026-10-26: "legend circles should always be hidden by scenery around them"; the grove's and the rim's south gap 0): a circle is hidden until she walks in, her see-through hole showing it then. Its **size** (Ed, 2026-10-26: "Legend circles need to be larger. Often legends don't fit in them"; "It only needs to fit them when they are sleeping"): each species' legend is measured from its art, asleep and awake, by `node tools/legends/footprint.mjs` into `config/legend-footprints.json` (`--check` says whether it's stale: run it after a creature's art changes); `rules/legendFit.ts` sizes each circle so the sleeping body, lying `top` x r north of the middle, fits with `legendClearing.fit.margin` to spare, never smaller than the species table gave it and at most `fit.max`; a tight area shrinks it in fine steps (`legendFit.test.ts`). The proof shot `legend:<n>[,<south>[,treetop]]` (`app/shot.ts`) puts her south of the nth biggest circle, looking in.
- **Legend area** (Ed, 2026-10-26: "legends are spawned in additional areas that have legend circles where runestones usually are"; "extra areas are added to each island, and the legend areas can be adjacent to the same type of area, so it effectively becomes an area twice the size"; all legends in them; "The route only cares about runestones"; islands grow): on an island by roster, every kind a night could deal there (the roster's but the island's own kind and placeholders: one each, chosen by the island's own seed, so its shape, land, coast and chart outline are the same every night) gets an area of its own grown out of the island's frontier beside one of its kind's areas, its **pair**: the roomiest such (the biggest circle it holds, up to its legend's need), never beside home, a lake or another legend area; it takes its pair's kind and look (`rules/map.ts`, `map.legendAreas`, `map.isLegendArea`). It's in the buffer (`inBuffer`: no runestone, soundsystem, arena, route stop, creatures or finds of its own), and the island's land and coast grow round it. Its legend's circle stands at its heart, sized to fit up to `legendClearing.fit.areaMax` (55 m: the elephant, 83 m asleep, gets what its area holds), clear of the runestones round it; the legend's own cell is its pair's, and `map.legendClearing(pair)` (or of the legend area) is its circle. Tonight's legends (dealt there, before the land: the early quest's areas taken as the three nearest home, their kinds choosing first) lie in theirs; the rest stand empty. A kind with no room beside any of its areas keeps its legend in one of its own areas, as before. **The pair partifies together** (Ed, 2026-10-26): `partifiedKey(p, map, key)` and `partyZones(p, map)` (`rules/party.ts`: the partified areas and each one's legend area as a zone of its own) for the string lights, the party border (round the outside of the pair), the canopy's uplight, the wisps, the combat light and the campfires; `party.areas` itself keeps the runestone areas (the floor's level and the music count them). **The pair counts as one area** (Ed: "for legends getting enraged, and also for animals attacking and defending, the adjacent areas should count as one"): `map.areaOf(cell)` / `map.sameArea` / `areaCellAt(map, x, z)`, used by combat's `inArea` (`game.ts creatureInArea`: edge, chase past it, defending, retreat), the legends' kin (`areaOf`, a sigil parked in the legend area counting for the pair), the wild watch and hunts, roused nappers, the hot areas stepped, quests (a sigil put down in a legend area answers its pair's legend) and clearing a wild area. `legends.areas` false: legends in their own areas as before. Tests: `rules/legendAreas.test.ts`.
- **Friendly area**: an area whose legend's quest is done before its wave: its creatures leave her and her party be while it's wild. There are no guards (Ed, 2026-10-06: "There are no more guards."). An area whose quest isn't done wakes **angry**.
- **Posse / travelling**: a party animal near the witch on the ground (about 60 m, `config/travel.json`) or at its sigil is her posse and fights (following her, it goes only for what's going for her or hers: first anything with her as its target, then the attacker fewest of hers are on, so they spread out, then the nearest, within `combat.pursuit` of her; only those within `combat.defendRadius` of her, "just ones that are in the same combat as you": a following one farther off fights back only at what's on it; Ed, 2026-10-09, `acquire` in rules/combat/targeting.ts; and a creature after her that one of them hits is **taunted**, turning on that one for `combat.taunt.time` seconds, a lunge at her already under way landing first, its wind-up's telegraph turning to face it: `c.taunt`, rules/combat/hits.ts); anywhere else it travels: quiet both ways, along area borders, its leash drawn as its route (eased between the rules' re-plans, `render/routeEase.ts`, so the dots glide rather than jump at treetop speed; `node tools/leash/route-strip.mjs [seed]` measures and draws it).
- **Conga dancer**: once the island is won, every animal but the woken legends dances in the line (`rules/win.ts`: `c.finale`, its place in it), round the dancefloor or, while she's on the ground in the home area, after her (`congaFollows`). It is never on her leash again (Ed's playtest, 2026-10-26: "When conga animals are following me, they shouldn't be leashed to me - they just follow me"; landing at home he'd found them on his stack, his sigil button picking up their runes): `inConga` (`rules/creatureStates.ts`) gives a dancer no rune, no 💌 or chat takes it, and `inviteCreature` refuses it. `win.test.ts` lands her at home and presses the button among the line: her stack stays empty.
- **Wild / happy / leashed / enraged**: a creature's state (#87, `src/rules/creatureStates.ts`): wild as found; happy once invited (defends its own area, dances at its soundsystem; its **sigil rune** at its feet, moving with it: Ed, 2026-10-06, `hasRune`; none for a happy legend); leashed once she picks that rune up with E (hers for good; `states.leash` "pickup", `pickupDelay`; the old second 💌 meter is "again"); enraged when a wave puts a soundsystem in its area (besieges). **Dazed**: a knocked-down wild creature, lying still a while (still invitable) before it runs off. Never "killed": "knocked down", "ran off".
- **Restless / angry legend, relic**: a legend whose area has none of its kind grows restless (a nightmare), then angry (it shoots her and her posse from afar); a relic (a giant half-buried party object, carried as a relic sigil) put down by a sleeping legend makes it happy (#87, `src/rules/legends.ts`).
- **Legend buff**: a legend's power, one of 30, each changing only her 💌s or her movement (`config/legend-buffs.json`, `rules/buffs.ts`). Legends give no powers (Ed, 2026-10-18: "Finishing quests no longer gives boons", nor from being happy or from flasks; 2026-10-24: nor a legend on her leash): `buffing` in `rules/legends.ts` is always false, the one switch that would turn them all back on (`partyLegends` in `rules/buffs.ts` asks it too). The data is kept for a possible return; the buff HUD now lights only for `?buffs=`. `?buffs=fox,toad,stag` forces some on.
