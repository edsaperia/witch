# The run

Part of Witch's notes (CLAUDE.md is the index): the phases of a run, the opening and the party spell, the clock, her controls and the camera, waves, knockouts and the party's end. Moved here from CLAUDE.md as it stood (housekeeping, Ed 2026-10-23: a note an area, so pull requests add to their own area's file, not one shared file); every rule kept. Add to this file, not CLAUDE.md, when you change this area.

**The phases of a run** (Ed's names, 2026-10-07), in order, and what marks each in the code:

- **Bedroom**: the character creator (`ui/creator.ts`), open at every load and the loading screen too: she walks her room (WASD, `ui/roomWalk.ts`) and dresses up while the forest grows behind it. `?creator=0` skips it (the tools and smoke runs).
- **Loading screen** (Ed, 2026-10-26; `src/loading.js`, `src/ui/loadingScreen.js`): over a fresh load into her bedroom, an empty starry sky where the World Tree and COVEN RUSH (Cinzel Decorative, OFL, `src/ui/fonts/`) appear in gold stars as the game loads (the load's marks and `view.readiness`), edge first; the heart's star last, at `witch:ready`, going supernova to white and clearing into the dark bedroom as the alarm goes off. Its knobs are the tuning's `loading` (the pause page's "#1194 Loading screen", remembered on the browser, read at the next load). `?loading=0` turns it off; it's skipped under automation unless `?loading=1`, and the bed's next night keeps its own dark sleep and dream.
- **The Cast**: the party spell (`ui/spellScroll.ts`): the scroll, clicked or Enter, casts it; `party.spellAt` turns from null to the time, and its burst (`PARTY_CAST`, 1.2 s) starts play. Until then the game waits (`awaitingSpell`, `heldBySpell`).
- **At the Decks**: she stands behind the DJ decks on the treehouse deck (`witch.seated`, `witch.perch`; the old treehouse's studio with `treehouse.deck.on` false), the camera in its opening close-up on the studio (`camera.intro`, `g.introFocus`). Nothing can reach her there. After a knockout she comes back here, at the game's own zoom (rules/knockout.ts).
- **Step-off**: her first move or rise: `seated` goes false (rules/witch.ts), the camera's intro eases out (`camera.intro.ease`), and `party.bootFrom` is set (rules/party.ts `stepParty`), once a run.
- **First Speaker**: about 3 s after the step-off (11.5 m at the boot pulse's 4 m/s, `boot.speed`), the boot's pulse reaches the home ring's first stone and it turns into a speaker (rules/bootRing.ts `bootPulseAt`).
- **The Boot**: the pulse goes round the home ring at its own speed (`boot.speed`, 4 m/s, the ley pulse's till Ed slowed the waves on 2026-10-18: the 179 m path in about 45 s, `bootSeconds`), each stone a speaker as it passes; no wave comes and no area grows, but the animals still fight (`party.bootUntil`).
- **First Countdown**: the boot done, the ley pulse sets off along the first link at its speed (`party.pulse`; `waveCountdown` is the metres left over the speed, `party.nextAt` its estimate): wave one lands as it reaches the first stone.
- **Wave One**: the first wave (`party.wave` 1, rules/party.ts `spreadWave`): every area next to home gets its soundsystem and is partified, its creatures wake, and the music moves to the arc's next step.
- **The Party's Over**: the home ring's speakers down (Ed, 2026-10-10; any soundsystem still standing switches off) (`g.over`): no end screen, the afterparty (`g.partyOver`, rules/partyOver.ts): the waves stop, nothing fights or hurts her, the creatures go home to sleep, and a small card under the clock says how long she lasted.

- **Game clock**: the time played, mm:ss from 0, top centre (Ed, 2026-10-06; `clockText`, `clockSeconds` in `rules/leypulse.ts`, `#clock` in `index.html`), from the party spell (`party.spellAt`: undefined, from the start of play; null, waiting at 00:00); it stops while the game is paused, and a soundsystem lost or the speakers coming up pop out under it. The wave timer bar on the right and the cue toward the dancefloor are gone (Ed, 2026-10-06).
- **The opening** (Ed, 2026-10-20; `app/opening.ts`, the storyboard and Ed's decisions in previews/ed/opening-mock/ on claude/ed-shots; on by default: the tuning's `opening.bedroom`, `?opening=bedroom|old` for one load): every first load but a chart's arrival (`?arrive=`), a bot game, `?creator=0` or `?zoo` (Ed, 2026-10-25, on `?seed=497831`: "it should be loading straight to her bedroom in the treehouse, not the character creation screen": a link naming its seed or island too, in that island's treehouse) is home's night, asleep in the dark (`render/wakeUp.ts` `WakeCard.sleep`) while it's made, then she wakes in her bedroom there (main.ts `wakeFirst`; Ed, 2026-10-23: "the first screen should be the witch waking up in her bedroom on the home island instead of the map": the sea chart came first from 2026-10-20 till then, and is the treehouse's map table's alone now; What's new and Options, its corner's then, are the pause page's, beside Controls: main.ts `pauseControls`, `ui/cornerPanel.ts`, the start card's own nodes borrowed and put back): `rules/opening.ts` `wakeInBedroom`, on the house's floor, the camera settled indoors; the **wake-up** (`rules/wakeUp.ts`, `g.wake`; the page's card, dark and her phone in `render/wakeUp.ts`, her poses in bed in `render/view/witch.ts`, the ring `chimes.ts` `alarm`, the knobs `opening.wake` and `sfx.alarm`): in bed with her phone's alarm ringing, E snoozing it (5 s, unlimited), moving getting her up (a stretch, then beside the bed), the dressing table's mirror opening by itself the first time (the oak's bedroom has no curtains: Ed, 2026-10-24; the island's art ready is only marked, `island:ready` for `?timing=1`); she wakes in her bed up in the Turret, beside the telescope (Ed, 2026-10-26: "Move the bed to the turret. The first night shouldn't keep her in the bedroom until she's dressed"; rules/wakeUp.ts `bedRoom`: the room the bed's in), held there only while she's in bed (asleep, the alarm, a snooze, the stretch: `doorShut`), then free to go anywhere dressed or not; her Bedroom, one room down, holds the dressing table, wardrobe and shoe rack and last night's clothes in their pile by the wardrobe (the cloak pegs in the Cloakroom, the bags in the Parlour), the bed and every spell key held off (never past the build); then the **DJ intro**: E at her decks (`atDecks`) seats her and casts the party spell there, its needle-drop routine starting the party (no other key casts it); the **shorter wake-up** (`startWake`'s `short`: later loads, `witch.woken` in localStorage, and after the bed, main.ts `nightInPlace`), any key getting her straight up; Controls on the pause page where the creator's tabs aren't set up (main.ts `pauseControls`). Its beats are `opening:*` marks for `?timing=1`; `node tools/opening/shots.cjs` shoots them. The bedroom's main-thread budget (2026-10-21): `node tools/opening/bedroom-budget.cjs [dist] [throttle] [secs]` (`OPENING=1` for the new opening, through to the mirror; `LIMIT=`) lists the long tasks from `witch:view` on. The creator never reads back a canvas the GPU drew (a read waits behind the game's frames: seconds each in the cloud): what it reads is made on CPU canvases (`cpuCanvas`), her frames' pixels kept once baked (`pixelsOf`), its room built a step a frame (`roomSteps`, render/slices.ts).
- **Party spell**: how a run starts (Ed, 2026-10-06: "you are standing behind your decks in your treehouse, there is a button on the screen that says "CAST THE PARTY SPELL", when you press it, the witch does a spellcasting animation, the pulse appears, and starts going around the dancefloor runestone ring, and you can start moving around"): `party.spellAt` null until then; then (Ed, 2026-10-06) the spell is a **scroll** with 🎶 on it, bottom right of the character creator (its panel on the left): nearing it the 🎶 glows, the paper ripples and trembles and the screen darkens; clicked (or Enter), it grows to fill the screen, crackles, bursts and is gone, about 1.8 s, and as that clears play starts with the spell cast (`src/ui/spellScroll.ts`, `#creator-start`; its sounds `hum`, `rustle`, `crackle`, `burst` in `platform/audio/spell.ts`, the tuning's `sfx.spell`; `node tools/smoke/spell-scroll.cjs` films it; prefers-reduced-motion tones it down); without the creator, Enter or her spell key (`castParty` in the controls; `castPartySpell` in `rules/party.ts`), nothing of hers moving and neither the game clock nor the boot-up running; cast, it's the game time (the renderer's hook for the ring's pulse), she plays the cast (the `liftSigil` pose and a burst of amber and rose sparkles) for `PARTY_CAST` seconds, still held, then can move. `?creator=0` (the tools and smoke runs) starts at once (`spellAt` undefined) unless `?spell=wait`; `?spell=auto` starts at once anywhere.
- **Nightie / clothes pile** (Ed, 2026-10-23: "When waking up she wears only a nightie; last night's clothes are in a pile on the floor (pick all up with x/e)"; `src/rules/clothesPile.ts`, `g.wake.pile`): on every wake-up (the first load's, later loads', after the bed) she's in her nightie (art/witch.js `nightie`, a white nightdress trimmed in her own colours, and `barefoot`), her outfit in a pile by her bed on every wake-up but her first (Ed, 2026-10-26: "if it's the first night, there won't be any last night's clothes": on the first, `wake.short` false, there's no pile, drawn or to use, `pileOut`; `pile` is still what she has to put on, each at its station, her guidance pins leading her round them, docs/notes/house.md **Guidance pins**) (the house object `clothesPile`, use `pile`; drawn from her own outfit, art/witch.js `clothesPileSprite`, of what's still in it), by piece: `top` (jacket, top, jeans), `shoes`, `cloak` (and scarf), `hat`, `bits` (headphones, glasses, bags...). E (X on the pad) at the pile puts all that's left on at once (the `dressed` wake event); a station's choice before then puts its own piece on her (`STATION_SLOT`: the wardrobe her top, the shoe rack her shoes, the cloak pegs her cloak; the dressing table nothing: her face and hair are hers in the nightie too) and out of the pile (`wearFromStation`, main.ts's stations.onLook). She's drawn in what she has on (`wornGenome`, given to the view by main.ts `showLook`). Dressed is the pile empty; the bedroom's door doesn't wait on it (Ed, 2026-10-25: "I should be able to leave regardless"): it's shut only till she's up (`doorShut`), and a hat she picks up off the hats' floor meanwhile comes out of the pile (`wearPiece`). `node tools/witch/nightie-sheet.mjs <png>` draws her nightie in every pose before she's dressed, the pile and her dressed.
- **Partified**: an area the party has reached: a soundsystem in its clearing, string lights round it, motes. Home is partified from the start.
- **Wave**: one step of the party spreading: one area per witch (`party.areasPerWave`), the next on the ley line's planned route, partified as the ley pulse reaches its stone (Ed, 2026-10-09: "Pure constant speed": `leyLines.pulseSpeed` 1 m/s (Ed, 2026-10-18; 4 before) times the tempo along the route's own curves, so each gap is its link's length over the speed; `rules/pulseRoute.ts`) after home's boot-up (N brings the next one, P pauses it); a fallen soundsystem doesn't move it (Ed, 2026-10-08). The pause page's pinned Waves slider sets it live, 0 (held) to 6 m/s, remembered on this browser (`config/decisions/953-waves.json`, `app/settings.ts`; Ed, 2026-10-18: the bedroom's dropdown gone); `?pulse=<m/s>|off` (and the old `?wave=off`) for one load, over it.
- **String lights**: lines of party bulbs hung between pairs of trees round a partified area's clearing.
- **Partified border**: the sparkling line round the outside of the partified region, in each area's sigil colour.
- **Sim step**: the rules advance only in fixed `STEP`s (1/60 s, `rules/game.ts`), driven only by inputs and the seed; `stepGame` runs as many as a frame makes up, and the view draws eased between the last two (`interpolated`). Rules code never reads the wall clock or `Math.random`.
- **Witches**: `game.witches[]`, one per player (body, leash, spell, dash, health, knockout); `game.witch`, `game.leash` and `game.spells` are the first's.
- **Knockout**: the witch at no hits left: her stack comes down bottom first, one every `knockout.releaseEach` seconds, each sigil put down where its animal stands, so they stay hers, parked (#87, 2026-10-05); her hat drops where she went down (`knockout.dropHat`, `rules/hat.ts`); then the teleport back behind her decks.
- **Party's over / afterparty**: how a run ends (Ed, 2026-10-06; `src/rules/partyOver.ts`): the home ring's speakers all down (Ed, 2026-10-10: not every soundsystem too; one still standing switches off, still and dark, its lights, lasers and hologram out with the rest, `render/party.ts`), `g.partyOver` (`{ at, ease }`, ease 0 to 1 over `partyOver.ease` seconds) replaces the old end screen. The waves stop, nothing fights or hurts her, her leash lets go, and every creature sleeps: those in her sight stroll home (`bed`, at `partyOver.walk` times their pace) and lie down, the rest (the ones that ran off too) are home asleep already. **Asleep** is the one shared sleep, `Creature.asleep` (a boolean, `asleepAt` when it lay down; a nap also sets `napUntil`, the party's over doesn't): still, out of every fight, no rune, no 💌s. `?partyover=1` starts it at once (`endParty`).

Its waves and boot wait as ever. As the spell's cast (her decks) everyone the night put to bed gets up where they lie, wild as ever, and the find's there. - **Dropped hat**: knocked out, she drops her hat where she went down (`rules/hat.ts`, Ed, 2026-10-06: "so you can go back and find it ... to help you not lose fights"; `knockout.dropHat`) and is drawn without it (the view's bare frames, `assets.witchBare()`, with the hat lying beside the spot, `witchHatSprite` in `art/witch.js`) until she stands on it and presses E, which puts it straight back on: never in the stack, and before any sigil or relic sigil under it. - **Controls** (Ed, 2026-10-06: "space for rise/land, E for place, Q for cycle. Mousewheel for zoom"; before that, 2026-10-05: "WASD and mouse. right click is dodge, q is up/down, e is place sigil/cycle"): WASD (or the arrows) move, the mouse aims and left click (or 1) throws 💌s, right click dodges (the dash), Space rises or lands, E puts down or picks up a sigil on the ground (`place`; also a happy creature's rune, a relic's sigil or her hat), Q cycles the stack anywhere (`cycle`), R her broom's spell if it has one (Speed's boost), the mouse wheel over the game zooms a step a notch (Z/X and +/− too); the browser's menu is off over the game; the gamepad and touch keep one sigil button (`sigil`: put down on the ground, cycle in the treetops). A dodge pressed up to `dash.buffer` seconds early waits and goes as soon as it can. The dodge goes toward the cursor (Ed's playtest, 2026-10-06; `dash.toCursor`), the way she faces with the cursor within `dash.aimDead` of her, and the way she steers with no cursor (touch); the right stick counts as a point 20 m out its way.
- **Glide**: the camera snaps to whole art pixels and the canvas is shifted by what's left in whole screen pixels (`view.subpixel`, `applyShake` in `main.ts`), so the art stays on its grid but the view glides. It follows the witch (her own snap and the camera's given back, so she holds still on screen to a pixel while the world lands within half an art pixel; Ed's playtest, 2026-10-06: "it feels low"). `node tools/feel/trace.cjs [seed] [area]` measures it: the built game driven frame by frame on 60, 120 and 144 Hz schedules through the real loop (`window.witch.frameLive`), where she, the ground and a creature land on screen.
- **Resting 💌s**: a 💌 in flight lies flat and spins like a frisbee (`invites.spin` turns a second; Ed, 2026-10-06), laid flat for the camera's pitch; one that met no one comes down with its puff, stops at the turn it landed at and lies flat on the ground `invites.linger` seconds, fading over the last `invites.lingerFade`, at most `invites.lingerMax` at once (Ed's playtest, 2026-10-06; `src/render/invites.ts`, drawn only: no hit, their images pooled).
- **Aim reticle**: the cursor over the game (`src/render/aimhud.ts`): a small ring, pink and whole where the ground under it is within a 💌's range (`invites.range`, buffed), dim and broken beyond, a dot alone in the treetops; a blue arc fills round it while the dodge recharges (the diamond marking where a dodge would land is gone: Ed, 2026-10-08).
- **Action bar**: the nine slots 1 2 3 4 Q E R, ␣ (Space) and RMB (the right mouse button) along the bottom (bindings in `platform/input.ts`: `KEYS`, `PAD`, `ACTION_BAR`).
