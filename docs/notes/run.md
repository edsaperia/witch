# The run

Part of Witch's notes (CLAUDE.md is the index): the phases of a run, the opening and the party spell, the clock, her controls and the camera, waves, knockouts and the party's end. Moved here from CLAUDE.md as it stood (housekeeping, Ed 2026-10-23: a note an area, so pull requests add to their own area's file, not one shared file); every rule kept. Add to this file, not CLAUDE.md, when you change this area.

Input reliability (2026-10-29, #1454): `platform/input.ts` reads every action's press before consuming keyboard/mouse edges, including Talk and Auto-talk. All three mouse buttons have held state; their down/up events track chords and releases over panels, while touch compatibility mouse events are ignored. `game.ts` queues hat presses with the other one-shot actions until the next fixed step and consumes each once, whether a rendered frame runs zero, one or several steps. Regression tests cover 30/60/144 fps, mouse holds for inviting/using (stump, bed and sigils), toggle bindings, chords, blur and start-screen consumption. Stump casting and the floor-hat rules are unchanged.

**The phases of a run** (Ed's names, 2026-10-07), in order, and what marks each in the code:

- **Bedroom**: by default (the opening, below) the wake-up in her Bedroom in the treehouse, dressing at its stations; with `?opening=old` the character creator (`ui/creator.ts`, main.ts `creator.show()` only when the load doesn't open in the bedroom), the loading screen too there: she walks her room (WASD, `ui/roomWalk.ts`) and dresses up while the forest grows behind it. `?creator=0` skips it (the tools and smoke runs).
- **Loading screen** (Ed, 2026-10-26; `src/loading.js`, `src/ui/loadingScreen.js`): over a fresh load into her bedroom, an empty starry sky where the World Tree and COVEN RUSH (Cinzel Decorative, OFL, `src/ui/fonts/`) appear in gold stars as the game loads (the load's marks and `view.readiness`), edge first; the heart's star last, at `witch:ready`, going supernova to white and clearing into the dark bedroom as the alarm goes off. Its knobs are the tuning's `loading` (the pause page's "#1194 Loading screen", remembered on the browser, read at the next load). `?loading=0` turns it off; it's skipped under automation unless `?loading=1`, and the bed's next night keeps its own dark sleep and dream.
- **The Cast**: the party spell: by default E at her decks (the opening's DJ intro, rules/wakeUp.ts); with `?opening=old` the scroll (`ui/spellScroll.ts`), clicked or Enter, casts it; `party.spellAt` turns from null to the time, and its burst (`PARTY_CAST`, 1.2 s) starts play. Until then the game waits (`awaitingSpell`, `heldBySpell`).
- **At the Decks**: she stands behind the DJ decks on the treehouse deck (`witch.seated`, `witch.perch`; the old treehouse's studio with `treehouse.deck.on` false), the camera in its opening close-up on the studio (`camera.intro`, `g.introFocus`). Nothing can reach her there. After a knockout she comes back here, at the game's own zoom (rules/knockout.ts).
- **Step-off**: her first move or rise: `seated` goes false (rules/witch.ts), the camera's intro eases out (`camera.intro.ease`), and `party.bootFrom` is set (rules/party.ts `stepParty`), once a run.
- **First Speaker**: about 3 s after the step-off (11.5 m at the boot pulse's 4 m/s, `boot.speed`), the boot's pulse reaches the home ring's first stone and it turns into a speaker (rules/bootRing.ts `bootPulseAt`).
- **The Boot**: the pulse goes round the home ring at its own speed (`boot.speed`, 4 m/s, the ley pulse's till Ed slowed the waves on 2026-10-18: the 179 m path in about 45 s, `bootSeconds`), each stone a speaker as it passes; no wave comes and no area grows, but the animals still fight (`party.bootUntil`).
- **First Countdown**: the boot done, the ley pulse sets off from where the boot's lap ended, on round the ring the same way and out along the first link (one path: docs/notes/view.md **One path**), at its speed (`party.pulse`; `waveCountdown` is the metres left over the speed, `party.nextAt` its estimate): wave one lands as it reaches the first stone.
- **Wave One**: the first wave (`party.wave` 1, rules/party.ts `spreadWave`): every area next to home gets its soundsystem and is partified, its creatures wake, and the music moves to the arc's next step.
- **The Party's Over**: the home ring's speakers down (Ed, 2026-10-10; any soundsystem still standing switches off) (`g.over`): no end screen, the afterparty (`g.partyOver`, rules/partyOver.ts): the waves stop, nothing fights or hurts her, the creatures go home to sleep, and the guidance pin leads her home from anywhere: 🧭 to the map table while there's an island found she hasn't been to, else 💤 to bed (rules/guidance.ts `guidanceOf`; docs/notes/house.md **Guidance pins**) (no card since Ed's 2026-10-26 "Remove the end cards"); once it's eased in, the night is recorded on the sea chart as played.

- **Game clock**: the time played, mm:ss from 0, top centre (Ed, 2026-10-06; `clockText`, `clockSeconds` in `rules/leypulse.ts`, `#clock` in `index.html`), from the party spell (`party.spellAt`: undefined, from the start of play; null, waiting at 00:00); it stops while the game is paused, and a soundsystem lost or the speakers coming up pop out under it.
