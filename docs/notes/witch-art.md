# The witch's art

Part of Witch's notes (CLAUDE.md is the index): the witch generator, the party witches and their pair poses, her lean cycle. Moved here from CLAUDE.md as it stood (housekeeping, Ed 2026-10-23: a note an area, so pull requests add to their own area's file, not one shared file); every rule kept. Add to this file, not CLAUDE.md, when you change this area.

- **Party witches**: the witches who fly in to the dancefloor, one for each active soundsystem (`partyWitch(seed)` in `art/witch.js`): generated witches (Ed, 2026-10-05: "the generated witches replace the party witches' outfits"), `witchGenome(seed, { party: true })` with two party neons for their glow sticks; the old outfits (`PARTY_OUTFITS`) still draw with `partyWitch(seed, { outfit })`. They and our witch share the party poses (dances, pairs, social, running, resting).
- **Pair poses**: party poses two witches do together (`WITCH_PAIRS`): each draws her own sprite, placed so their `pair` anchors meet (the partner mirrored, so they face each other; the conga's hands on the `back` of the witch ahead; the twirl's partner spins under the raised hand; the broom limbo is two witches holding a broom level and a third leaning back under it).
- **Witch genome / witch generator**: a witch as genome data (`art/witchGenome.js`): her hat (shape: the witch's classic, crooked, floppy, small and flowers, and the creator's none, top, cowboy, conical, boppers, party, musketeer, wizard, beanie, crown, mushroom and traffic; crown height, brim, lean, band), hair, top, cloak (none, short, long, hooded), broom (classic, fan, twig or round bristles; handle length and bend; bristle length; and the creator's other kinds, Ed 2026-10-06, `art/brooms.js`: missile, curled tip, handlebars, hobby horse, jet bike, oar, mop, pitchfork, canoe, ladder, speeder bike, bicycle, quad drone and gyrocopter (the wicker basket left out), each in the Broom box's two colours, body and trim, its flames, exhausts and lights glowing, its rotors, wheels and drips turning with the frame; she sits in the canoe, and on foot the vehicles stand beside her; `node tools/witch/brooms-sheet.mjs <png> [kinds]` and `brooms-gif.mjs` draw them; generated witches keep the four brooms), cloak length and patches, scarf length (0 none to 7, streaming far behind her; Ed, 2026-10-06: "allow a longer cloak and a longer scarf": past ×3 it flutters with the frame), the cloak up to ×6 (past the old lengths a train of cloth streaming and rippling behind her in flight, down her back and along the ground on foot; its train `extra`, so it neither shrinks nor lifts her; `node tools/witch/longwear-sheet.mjs <png>` draws the old and new ends), bag size and backpack size (0 none), the sliders' ranges wider than a generated witch's (Ed, round 11: "the sliders ... should go further"; `node tools/witch/hats-sheet.mjs` and `sliders-sheet.mjs` draw them), accessories both witchy and modern (Ed: a lit lantern at her hip, a glowing potion vial and a spellbook at her belt; headphones, sunglasses, round or not, glow sticks, a festival wristband, a bum bag, chunky trainers; a scarf, a satchel, a glowing pendant, earrings) and palette (per part, as `DEFAULT_OUTFIT`). `genomeLook(g)` gives the look and outfit `witchSprite` draws; `WITCH_GENOME` is ours and draws her exactly; `witchGenome(seed)` is a new witch within the limits in `WITCH_AXES` (narrower, `WITCH_RANDOM`: a witch's pointed hat two times in three, else one of the creator's, rarely none, always with its glowing band, and one of the four brooms; brims up to nearly twice hers, a floppy one's to 1.5, and cloaks mostly long, up to 1.75 times).
- **Lean cycle**: the witch's ordinary flying speed at ground level as a 4-frame loop (`pose: "lean"`), played faster with speed.
