# Combat movement and mixed teams — #1622

Ed's 2026-11-01 instructions supersede the original experiment: "ship when ready, don't preserve", "do both together", and "Ship an early version before testing with cloud fights."

The early version combines numerical waiting movement, mixed-species team roles, youth weighting and damage withdrawal. Shared paths use radius, period, radial waves/lobes, stretch, heading following, formation offsets and a return envelope before attacks. Locomotion uses speed, cadence/duty/rest speed, stride, lift and turning multipliers. Steering and signature charge/leap/burrow/swoop trajectories use the same numerical integrator in rules/locomotion.ts; routes interpolate continuously with signed bend and momentum carry. All 64 species have distinct resolved waiting profiles, with deliberate variation among the ten home-island animals. Close passes use range bias and loop-centre displacement. Movement alone causes no damage.

Teams group fighting youths/adults by side and target, including mixed posse members. One encounter clock controls approach, tell, attack window and recovery for all its species; the previous species-combo clock runs only for private baseline diagnostics. Roles are seeded weighted choices among available melee/ranged/special capabilities, capped at one melee, two ranged, one special and four active. Species tempo affects opportunity priority and encounter pace independently of role weights or aim quality. Home tempos range from slower snail/badger to quicker dormouse/stoat. Committed attacks finish before a phase transition or damage withdrawal; an all-hurt team retains an attacker. Babies and legends are excluded from team membership; waiting members react to hostile legend telegraph geometry. Passive movement effects observe the completed movement, including during waits.
