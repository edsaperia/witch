# Combat movement and mixed teams — #1622

Ed's 2026-11-01 instructions supersede the original experiment: "ship when ready, don't preserve", "do both together", and "Ship an early version before testing with cloud fights."

The early version combines numerical waiting movement, mixed-species team roles, youth weighting and damage withdrawal. Shared paths use radius, period, radial waves/lobes, stretch, heading following, formation offsets and a return envelope before attacks. Locomotion uses speed, cadence/duty/rest speed, stride, lift and turning multipliers. Steering and signature charge/leap/burrow/swoop trajectories use the same numerical integrator in rules/locomotion.ts; routes interpolate continuously with signed bend and momentum carry. All 64 species have distinct resolved waiting profiles, with deliberate variation among the ten home-island animals. Close passes use range bias and loop-centre displacement. Movement alone causes no damage.

Teams group fighting youths/adults by side and target, including mixed posse members. One encounter clock controls approach, tell, attack window and recovery. Ed's later 2026-11-01 ruling removes the universal special role: config/creature-actions.json defines an arbitrary list of actions per species, each with a weight, youth multiplier, optional cooldown, range and executor parameters. The scheduler draws actions, not delivery slots; four animals may be admitted with the same delivery, subject to actual strike pressure. Each actor/action has its own readiness timestamp. There is no reserved special slot, mandatory third action, or species-wide special cooldown in the new model. Ram knockback is part of its chosen melee charge; stampede is coordinated charge movement rather than an extra charge choice, and harmless feints come from waiting paths. Legacy pack role labels remain only at the existing mechanical executor adapter and in private diagnostic code. Species tempo affects opportunity priority and encounter pace independently of action weights or aim quality. Home tempos range from slower snail/badger to quicker dormouse/stoat. Committed actions finish before a phase transition or damage withdrawal; an all-hurt team retains an attacker. Babies and legends are excluded from team membership; waiting members react to hostile legend telegraph geometry. Passive movement effects observe completed movement, including during waits.
