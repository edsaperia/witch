# Haunted music: risks and follow-up ideas (8 Nov 2026)

The haunted treatment (Ed, 8 Nov, "B, 1-6") puts the Chimera singer in the quiet sections and the legend circles, with:
- its own long, dark reverb;
- held notes and slow chords;
- a ghost voice a sixth below;
- a drone;
- Phrygian ♭2 and harmonic-minor colour, an exception to dorian in those sections only;
- a voice lift.

The drums are silent at first, then drum & bass drops in on a phrase line. There are no drums in legend circles.

Ed, after hearing the ideas below: "let's see what the builder makes and then revisit", and "Note the risks too". This page keeps both for that review.

**Why it fits:** Ed's mood references are Grimes' "4ÆM" and "Venus Fly": ethereal layers crashing into hard drums and breakbeats. "4ÆM" itself floats a vocal, then drops into jungle.

## Risks to check in the builder's renders

1. **Tempo.** Drum & bass sits at about 170–175 bpm; the game's records run 100–200. A literal double time at 120 bpm is 240 and frantic. Pick the pattern by tempo:
   - native two-step at 160 bpm and up;
   - fast 16th hats over a half-time snare below that, which still feels like drum & bass.
2. **Mud.** A 5 s voice reverb under breakbeats smears the mix. Duck the voice's reverb on the kick and snare, or shorten it while the drums play.
3. **Overuse.** If every breakdown goes haunted and drops, it stops being special. Keep it rare and earned.
4. **The scale exception spreading.** The ♭2 and raised 7th must stay inside the haunted sections. Elsewhere it's dorian, by Ed's 9 Oct ruling, and tests should enforce that.
5. **Cost.** An extra reverb and the drone must not stall the music's render-ahead on Ed's Intel laptop. Check with the stall test.

## Ideas to revisit (best first)

1. **Make the drop land.** In the drumless stretch:
   - the drone's filter slowly opens;
   - a reversed swell of the voice's reverb rises into the downbeat;
   - one beat of near-silence just before the hit.
2. **A reese bass in the drop:** detuned saws growling on the drone's root. It gives the drop weight and suits the ♭2 colour.
3. **The voice joins the drop:** keep the long notes, and add occasional rhythmic 16th chops of the same Chimera notes.
4. **Escalate through the night:** a light, liquid first drop, and heavier, jungle-style breaks later, fitting Ed's "each night a full set, building to a climax".
5. **Tie it to play:** the quiet stretch while a wave gathers, the drop as it arrives. The music already has build hooks into waves.
6. **Legend circles as hymns:** no drums, and the voice sings a slow motif of the legend's own, perhaps blended toward the child voice to sound older and stranger.
