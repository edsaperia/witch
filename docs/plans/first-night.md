2. **Evidence labels.** **Code** means behavior traced in this revision, including its configured switches; it does not establish visual clarity. **Observed (handoff)** means the coordinator's actual browser observations, transferred to this reviewer, not independently reproduced here. **Hypothesis** means a likely newcomer difficulty to test, not a confirmed defect. **Proposal** means a possible future change requiring Ed's approval. Severity ranks the consequence *if the hypothesis occurs*: **high** blocks the core loop or teaches a wrong rule; **medium** causes avoidable wandering or retries; **low** adds friction without blocking. These are provisional priorities, not measured failure rates.

## Current newcomer journey and risks

4. **Load and arrive.** Observed (handoff): stars gradually formed the COVEN RUSH logo and eventually the bedroom loaded at v5639. Loading took several minutes on a busy 16 GB laptop with other checks running. This is one contaminated observation, not a general performance result. A short console query found no warning/error; it is not a full-session console check. **Hypothesis, medium:** a newcomer may not know whether loading is progressing. Measure first usable frame on a fresh runner and ordinary playtest devices before proposing performance work or different progress feedback.

5. **Wake and move.** Observed (handoff): WASD keycaps beside the bed and E snooooze were visible; S began “*yaaawn*…*stretch*”, then movement worked. The witch walked south a short way; clock 00:00, 120 BPM. Code: [`stepWake`](../../src/rules/wakeUp.ts) makes movement start stretching, E snooze, then opens the bedroom after the stretch. **Hypothesis, medium:** a player who assumes E means get up can repeatedly snooze. Existing differentiated prompts should first be tested: count snoozes and time to voluntary movement. The three-second stretch is intentional, not evidence of an input failure.

6. **Follow the first house pin.** Observed (handoff): an icon/distance cue decreased from 68 m to 60 m after southward movement. Its destination was not reached. Code: [`FIRST_MORNING` / `guidanceOf`](../../src/rules/guidance.ts) supplies one target at a time: dressing table (skin/hair), wardrobe (jacket/top/trousers), shoes, bag, invitations, cloak, hat, lathe/broom, then decks (with unseen finds considered before decks). [`GuidePin`](../../src/render/view/guidance.ts) keeps the icon above its target and at the screen edge when away; morning pins already pulse. **Hypothesis, medium:** a straight destination bearing across several connected rooms may be mistaken for a walkable route. Check turns, wrong branches and whether distance reliably helps. Do not add words to the pin or claim pulsing is missing.

7. **Open, choose and close a station.** Code: [`stations.ts`](../../src/rules/stations.ts), [`stepSigilButton`](../../src/rules/sigilButton.ts), and [`StationPanel`](../../src/ui/stationPanel.ts) implement the nearby object cue, E to open a side panel and menu interaction. Opening the dressing table marks the styling step; other first-morning steps depend on clothes/kit state. Older backlog descriptions of menus appearing automatically on approach and disappearing as she walks away are superseded by the 26 October E/menu ruling. **Hypothesis, medium:** a newcomer may confuse highlighting a choice with wearing it, or keep trying to walk while the panel owns movement keys. Observe the selected appearance, successful close action and next pin. Customisation is expressive play; measure time voluntarily spent choosing separately from time stuck.

8. **Collect functional kit among optional clothes.** Code: [`kit.ts`](../../src/rules/kit.ts) starts a normal waiting night without invitations or broom. A bag is required to take invitations; one E takes an unlimited night's supply. One E at the lathe takes a broom and also opens its station. A knockout retains kit; a new night resets it. **Hypothesis, high:** a player may assume every cosmetic stop is mandatory, or mistake looking at the lathe for taking the broom. Check whether she can tell what she carries and whether she knows invitations do not run out. Preserve optional dressing and freedom to leave; do not make finishing the tour a condition of play.

9. **Leave early, or start the party.** Code: [`doorShut` / `startParty`](../../src/rules/wakeUp.ts) restrict waking, not clothing completion. At the decks E opens the DJ booth; choosing its existing “Get this party started” action starts the spell at the selected record's tempo. She can leave beforehand: [`worldAsleep`](../../src/rules/partyOver.ts) leaves creatures asleep and uninvitable, quests closed and the night's find absent until the spell. Without a broom, stepping off the Terrace causes a fall and return to the magic circle. **Hypothesis, high:** sleeping forest exploration may look like the intended game, or a fall may feel like failure without a recoverable cause. Test whether the persistent unfinished house pin and visible kit objects bring her back. Do not teach Enter/the old creator scroll as the normal opening, or automatically start the party to mask confusion.

10. **Understand what starting changed.** Code: [`party.ts`](../../src/rules/party.ts), [`bootRing.ts`](../../src/rules/bootRing.ts), wake-up and asleep-world rules start the home ring's transformation, music and later route pulse. The DJ spell starts the night; a stump's soundsystem spell expands its party area. **Hypothesis, high:** two musical spells and similar music-note cues can collapse into one unexplained action. Observe whether she notices the world waking and can identify the next useful destination without coaching. The existing spectacle is a teaching opportunity; its presence alone is not proof she understands it.

11. **Approach a wild pack and invite.** Code: [`stepInvites`](../../src/rules/invites.ts), [`affectionOf`](../../src/rules/game.ts), [`hit`](../../src/rules/affection.ts) and [`orbit.ts`](../../src/rules/orbit.ts) govern thrown invitations and heart slots. On the ground, invitations fill slots; a full wild non-legend is **stunned**, and the **next invitation while stunned befriends it**. Combat hearts can contribute to those slots too. Enraged creatures cannot be invited. **Hypothesis, high:** the full slots and stun may look like completed recruitment, so she stops throwing one invitation too soon. Test the existing stun and celebration feedback before adding a cue. Do not teach the obsolete rule “full meter immediately makes it happy.”

12. **Read the stump's stack and make it ready.** Code: [`stoneStack` / `castReady`](../../src/rules/stoneStacks.ts) keeps invited creatures' sigils in their area's stack; there is no rune at their feet at this stage. Friends' fighters are above enemies; babies stay at the top, purple regardless of side; non-baby friends use blues, enemies reds, with rank shown by size/intensity and legend shine. Every remaining wild youth/adult must be invited before she can manually cast; babies and a sleeping legend do not block it. **Hypothesis, high:** chasing a newly friendly animal for a pickup prevents learning conversion, or she mistakes purple babies for remaining enemies. Measure whether she checks the stack, identifies the remaining hostile fighters and finds the newly available spell spot. Do not explain readiness as “every animal,” which incorrectly includes babies and legends.

13. **Cast the soundsystem spell on the stump.** Code: configured `cast.on` and `runeMarkers.stump.on` are both true in [`tuning.json`](../../config/tuning.json); fallback comments saying stacks are off are stale. [`castSpot` / `castingStone` / `stepCast`](../../src/rules/stoneStacks.ts) use the stump's centre with this switch, not the old spot 4 m south. Standing on it on foot, hold the sigil/use button; configured cast time is **4 seconds**, not the older six-second prose. Progress drains when not casting; a hit interrupts and requires releasing then holding again. The rendered music/analyser ring already exists. **Hypothesis, high:** a tap, the wrong position, or holding through an interruption may seem broken. Observe whether the existing spot, ring and loss feedback communicate hold, progress and recovery. There is no automatic clearing just because the animals are gone.

14. **Pick up a sigil and bring an animal.** Code: conversion scatters friend sigils as [`LooseSigil`](../../src/rules/stoneStacks.ts) entries; E on a loose sigil leashes its animal through [`stepSigilButton`](../../src/rules/sigilButton.ts) and [`leash.ts`](../../src/rules/leash.ts). A legend's loose sigil is a tether to the sleeper, not a mobile recruit; the circle baby's sigil lands in its circle. **Hypothesis, high:** celebrating animals and scattered symbols compete for attention, or the first chosen legend sigil unexpectedly constrains movement. Measure noticing a loose ordinary sigil, pickup and a visible follower. Teach the common non-legend case first; do not promise that every loose sigil behaves alike.

15. **Place and retrieve a defender.** Code: [`leash.ts`](../../src/rules/leash.ts), [`sigilButton.ts`](../../src/rules/sigilButton.ts), [`travel.ts`](../../src/rules/travel.ts) support putting down the selected carried sigil, guarding near it, picking it back up and followers travelling when away. The same use action handles several contexts. **Hypothesis, high:** the player sees a collectible rather than a movable order to an animal, or cannot identify which carried sigil will be placed. Observe one deliberate placement, the animal's response and deliberate retrieval; distinguish accidental drops. Teach cycling only once she carries more than one. Keep this review separate from #1456's controller implementation.

16. **Connect waves to defence, escape and recovery.** Code: the ley pulse converts the next area even if its pack is not manually ready; remaining wild fighters become enraged and attack soundsystems. [`homeDown`](../../src/rules/partyOver.ts) ends the party when **all home speakers** are down, even if outlying soundsystems stand. Rising and dodging are distinct actions; ground invitations need landing, and landing has a rise cooldown. Knockout is recoverable. **Hypothesis, high:** a new player follows the attractive pulse without recognising an incoming threat, keeps inviting enraged creatures, or thinks losing one outlying soundsystem ends the run. Test a clear threat and one recovery rather than teaching every combat modifier. Exact pad prompts/layout are #1456's responsibility; shared timing and per-witch behavior are #1457's.

17. **Finish a night and choose another.** Code: [`win.ts`](../../src/rules/win.ts) handles route completion/stragglers, victory pulses and the party; the win settles when she reaches the dancefloor. [`guidanceOf`](../../src/rules/guidance.ts) then points to the chart while an available island has not been visited, otherwise bed. A loss gives a safe sleeping forest and homeward guidance. Hold E at bed to sleep and begin another night; a tap is not enough. No end cards. Legend quests find islands for tonight; winning keeps them, winning alone finds none. **Hypothesis, medium:** free wandering looks like an unfinished objective, or a player waits for an absent game-over screen. Check finding home, interpreting the final pin and sleeping/sailing without instructions. Teach legends, finds on loan, berries and the wider campaign after the core recruit/defend loop, when encountered; do not front-load a manual.

## Proposed teaching sequence: world first, text last

18. **Wake, use one object, retain agency.** Proposal: start by testing the existing wake prompts and one-pin morning route unchanged. Use the dressing table as the safe first example of approach → nearby use cue → open → choose/close. Make later adjustments to cue visibility or room sightlines only where a recording shows a missed action. Keep Ed's exact station order; keep optional clothing, early exit and no wake-up tip card. A distance pin locates an object; doors and walkways must carry route information.

19. **Know what she is taking.** Proposal: use the existing bag, invitation piles and three physical brooms as the functional preparation lesson. Confirm each pickup is legible on the witch/world before considering stronger pickup feedback. Let optional styling remain enjoyable. If the first playtest finds repeated silent attempts without kit, investigate existing refusal/kit feedback first; any new local wording is a last resort for Ed, never a label on guidance pins.

20. **Start something she can see and hear.** Proposal: have the decks' existing button and opening performance explain “I started this party.” Evaluate whether the first useful ground destination is readable from the Terrace through the existing pulse, music, stump and pointer. Teach takeoff/landing through the first trip; expose dodge at the first actual attack. Do not introduce a second detached controls screen before she can act.

21. **Complete one recruit loop before broad strategy.** Proposal: select a representative early area for the playtest and watch invite → slots full/stun → one more invite → friendly stack → spell available → stand/hold → soundsystem rises → loose sigil → pickup/follower. First use existing effects. If a transition repeatedly fails, strengthen that transition's local shape, motion, sound or proximity cue rather than adding a global checklist. This is a teaching order, not a guarantee that all procedurally generated first packs currently offer it, and not an approved special tutorial island.

22. **Use that recruit for one understandable defence.** Proposal: demonstrate through consequences that placing a sigil positions its animal and retrieving it lets it follow again. Let the next threat show why that matters. Observe a safe retreat and a recoverable knockout if one occurs naturally. Only introduce multiple-sigil cycling when the player has a second recruit. The success criterion is an intentional decision, not button-press completion.

23. **Let the night resolve, then open the larger game.** Proposal: use the existing changing music, victory/afterparty and chart/bed pin. Introduce a legend quest or find on encounter after the ordinary loop is understood, not as a prerequisite to the first conversion. If players need words, use the existing local object/quest interface for the specific misunderstanding. Do not restore the removed tip or end cards.

## Playtest objectives and measurements

29. **Test setup and validity.** Use a genuinely empty browser profile/context on a runner that can launch, then the same captured build/seed/world for a reproducible baseline. Separately try another seed to avoid overfitting the route. Record version SHA, link switches, viewport, input device, performance rung, device/browser and participants' relevant experience. Normal opening only: no `creator=0`, `kit=full`, automatic spell, paused waves or debug transport in a newcomer trial. `newWitch=1` ignores character saves; it does **not** prove an empty browser profile or reset every preference/campaign record. Do not clear Ed's personal storage. Keep the one-look test world separate from his campaign.

30. **Collect task evidence, not a single completion time.** For each journey step record first cue seen, first attempted action, successful action, wrong attempts, idle interval, backtracks, help source and the participant's own explanation. Record real time and game time separately: preparation waits and house slow-time make them incomparable. Distinguish voluntary styling/exploration from being stuck. Suggested intervention rule for a baseline: after 60 seconds without progress ask what they are trying to do; after another 60 offer the smallest useful hint and mark that transition assisted. These are proposed observation rules, not gameplay timers or established targets.

31. **Primary outcomes.** Unaided time to wake; time to open/close the first station; ability to acquire bag/invites/broom; intentional party start; first befriend after stun; first manual conversion; first ordinary sigil pickup; first intentional placement/retrieval; first defence; recovery from confusion/knockout; reaching bed/chart after the outcome. Report numerator/denominator for each transition, plus every intervention and abandoned attempt. With a small social playtest use counts and recordings, not claims of statistically established percentages.

32. **Understanding checks.** At a natural pause ask “What changed when that animal stopped moving?”, “What has to happen before you can do the spell here?”, “How would you bring that animal with you?”, and “What are you protecting?” Do not teach the answer in the question. Watch whether they can repeat the loop in a second area with less help. A lucky conversion/pickup without an accurate model does not pass independent understanding.

33. **Social checks, once multiplayer is runnable.** Record which player sees which preparation pin, whether a friend's pickup changes another's guidance/kit, who starts the party, whether an unready guest understands the transition, help offered by friends, and whether one experienced player completes every learning action. These are test requirements/proposals, not claims that host/guest networking works at this revision. Reconcile with #1457's delivered rules and #1456's final mappings before building a multiplayer session script.

34. **Proposed readiness criterion for a teaching change.** In the next small newcomer round, each participant should complete and explain one recruit/conversion/placement loop, then repeat the key actions without a direct instruction. Report failures individually and revise the specific transition. Agree any time or success-rate target after the baseline; do not invent a pass threshold from the single partial laptop observation. Separately record errors, crashes and load times so product comprehension is not confused with device failures.
