## Recognition checklist

- Broad antler palms with teeth around their rims
- Long overhanging nose
- Throat bell
- Tall shoulder hump
- Long shins with narrow ankles
- Rust-brown, antlerless calf

## Proportions and graph

Adult modelling ranges use shoulder-to-rump length as 1. In neutral stance: shoulder joint to ground 1.03–1.25, skull back to nose 0.42–0.53, shoulder to poll along the neck 0.27–0.40, tail root to tip 0.05–0.10. These are approximate art targets inferred from silhouettes, not measured biological ranges. Head length excludes horns, antlers, tusks and trunk.

Priority order: Build palm blades → Drop the muzzle → Raise the withers. Graph gap at #1866 revision `0a884af6`: Broad antler membranes with separate rim tines, a pendant throat bell and a drooping nose pad.
