## Fit and variety review

Read canonical6707981581 and later6707992824 in full: crystals, flames and
spirit wings are allowed when they fit. None is rejected merely by category.
The stegosaur lava-plate option is a valid alternative; B better emphasizes
epochs of age and avoids an automatic fire motif for every ancient reptile.
Newt's glowing sail remains its breeding membrane, not added generic flames.

Within L7, renewal repeats only as different animal processes: python sheds a
dead outer skin, salamander regrows living tissue. These use different anatomy,
materials and silhouettes. Resonance repeats in rattlesnake/toad because one
is a keratin tail instrument and one is a vocal throat membrane. Neither gets
the other's effect origin or body shape. No lane-wide jewellery, crown, wings,
crystal forest or fire crest is assigned.
