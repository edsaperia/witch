## Variety review

Within this lane, tail-led concepts are deliberately different: fox **memory in fur whorls**, Arctic fox **shelter/insulation**, red panda **balance and misty ledges**. They are three real tail-bearing animals, not three copies of the same magical plume. If the global set already overuses great tails, fox B is the first to reconsider against its single-tail alternative's likeness; do not silently decide Ed's choice.

Only fennec B uses ember/flame as its signature, located inside its own ear cups and tied to the erg's old hearth. Arctic fox B's crystals are a valid alternative, not banned, but A was stronger on insulation/story. Wolf A uses the howl; raccoon A uses clever touch; badger A uses worked digging claws; otter C uses tactile current sensing. No selected concept relies on a generic crown, full armour uniform or accessory necklace.
