**Proposed pick C: Dusk held in hoofprints.** A single ring of giant hoofprints holds different moments of dusk in each print: sunset, twilight, stars. Small spectral herds pass between the prints below the giant’s legs. Preserve a slight chest and short rack. The prints must originate at the split hooves and stay behind when the buck moves.

## Recognition checklist

- Short upright antlers close to forehead
- Three points per mature beam
- Large lateral ears
- Narrow jaw ending in a black nose
- Pale rump with barely visible tail
- Small spotted fawn

## Proportions and graph

Adult modelling ranges use shoulder-to-rump length as 1. In neutral stance: shoulder joint to ground 0.95–1.18, skull back to nose 0.30–0.39, shoulder to poll along the neck 0.36–0.48, tail root to tip 0.01–0.04. These are approximate art targets inferred from silhouettes, not measured biological ranges. Head length excludes horns, antlers, tusks and trunk.

Priority order: Keep rack compact → Separate ears from beams → Expose pale rump. Graph gap at #1866 revision `0a884af6`: Tune the existing cervid head, spots and short tail. An individually directed rear tine is the principal antler gap.
