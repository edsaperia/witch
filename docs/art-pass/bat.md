## Identifying checklist

1. Fox-like muzzle and dark nose, large dark eyes.
2. Small ears; no giant microbat ears or nose-leaf.
3. Golden shoulder/neck mantle over dark furry torso (shipped identity).
4. Forelimbs transformed into elbow/wrist/finger-supported membrane wings.
5. Free thumb claws, two hind feet; no extra forelegs.
6. Level/path-angled flight torso, forward head, hindfeet trailing or tucked.
7. Folded membranes wrap torso while hanging/crouched; juvenile bigger head, shorter muzzle, smaller wing span.

Small torso with shoulder/pectoral emphasis between large thin wings. Head leads. Folded membrane should compress around body; flight must not retain a standing vertical chest.
