## Recognition checklist

- Heavy horn roots beside crown
- Horns coil beside cheeks and turn outward
- Short muzzle below the coil
- Compact neck with fleece ruff
- Sturdy legs with dark split hooves
- Hornless lamb with conspicuous ears

## Proportions and graph

Adult modelling ranges use shoulder-to-rump length as 1. In neutral stance: shoulder joint to ground 0.75–0.96, skull back to nose 0.31–0.41, shoulder to poll along the neck 0.17–0.28, tail root to tip 0.07–0.13. These are approximate art targets inferred from silhouettes, not measured biological ranges. Head length excludes horns, antlers, tusks and trunk.

Priority order: Open the horn coil → Frame the muzzle → Keep lamb ears visible. Graph gap at #1866 revision `0a884af6`: Tapered spiral horn with controlled clearance around the cheek; fleece volume independent of the skull.
