## Recognition checklist

- Heavy horn roots beside crown
- Horns coil beside cheeks and turn outward
- Short muzzle below the coil
- Compact neck with fleece ruff
- Sturdy legs with dark split hooves
- Hornless lamb with conspicuous ears
