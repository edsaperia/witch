## Identifying checklist

1. Long forward neck and elongated small tapered head.
2. Low shoulder line without brown-bear muscular hump.
3. Small round ears against creamy white fur.
4. Dark nose/eyes in long narrow face.
5. Large broad paws and heel-down rear feet.
6. Long low torso and heavy rounded rump.
7. Tiny tail; cub has shorter face/neck and relatively bigger head.

## Body plan

Adult: head 0.23–0.30B; exposed neck 0.25–0.40B; support height 0.55–0.75B; tail 0.04–0.08B. Cub: head 0.30–0.40B, neck 0.15–0.25B, lower compact torso.

Elongated trunk flows through neck into head; bulk spread through trunk and rump, not a grizzly hump or a white dog chest.

All ratios use torso B from shoulder to rump excluding head, neck and tail; limb-chain length and standing support height are different quantities. Ranges are authored starting targets, not biological measurements inferred from generated pictures. Tune against real anatomy after a graph render.
