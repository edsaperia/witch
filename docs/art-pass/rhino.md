## Recognition checklist

- Long low heavy head
- Broad square grazing lip
- Large nasal horn ahead of a shorter horn
- High shoulders
- Rounded swivelling ears
- Three-toed feet
- Calf has a small muzzle and undeveloped horns

## Proportions and graph

Adult modelling ranges use shoulder-to-rump length as 1. In neutral stance: shoulder joint to ground 0.68–0.85, skull back to nose 0.40–0.52, shoulder to poll along the neck 0.17–0.29, tail root to tip 0.20–0.30. These are approximate art targets inferred from silhouettes, not measured biological ranges. Head length excludes horns, antlers, tusks and trunk.

Priority order: Lower the head → Stagger nasal horns → Split three front toes. Graph gap at #1866 revision `0a884af6`: Two independently sized midline nasal horn sockets and a three-toed padded foot. Keep the elder’s single enlarged horn as a separate game form.
