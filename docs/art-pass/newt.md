## Identifying features

1. Long slim low torso, much less stocky than the fire salamander.
2. Small flattened head and a short, narrow neck transition.
3. Four delicate splayed legs, four front digits and five hind, no claws.
4. Long laterally compressed tail with a thin vertical fin outline.
5. Breeding adult's thin serrated back crest, separated from tail crest at base.
6. Dark granular skin, pale flecks and orange belly; no reptile scales.
7. Juvenile has no adult sail, no external gills after metamorphosis, and a
   relatively larger head on a much less bulky body.

must not turn into a row of thick dinosaur plates or spikes. Torso and tail
need independent cross-sections, with a narrow flexible skin crest on top.
