Owned species: beetle, crab, dragonfly, moth, scorpion, snail, spider, woodlouse. Docs/reference only; no graph, creature source, gameplay or activation changes.

| [spider](spider.md) | Brachypelma hamorii/redknee comparison; current genome specifies hairy orange-banded tarantula | Separate eight walking legs and palps → expose two body masses → keep orange joint bands | No tarantula family in #1866; generic three mammal-leg families lack radial multi-joint spider articulation, front pedipalps/chelicerae and a narrow pedicel connecting a separate abdomen.
