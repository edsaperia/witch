## Identifying features

1. Compact stout snake with recognisable narrow neck behind a broad wedge head.
2. Raised small scales above eyes, not horns or antlers on ordinary adults.
3. Sandy ground colour, dark dorsal blotches and keeled scales.
4. Short narrow terminal tail, sharply distinguished from the thick midbody.
5. Segmented terminal rattle, not a ball or single long cone.
6. Limbless coils at rest; lateral travelling contact arcs in sidewinding.
7. Neonate has a button, not an adult stack; large stacks are not a reliable
   biological age counter because shedding and breakage vary.
