# Rattlesnake (sidewinder) — skeleton target

Target Crotalus cerastes body plan: the current genome explicitly says desert
sidewinder. Adult and neonate level0, toward/away, resting/sliding. Not a cobra
or python recolour. [Method](l7-method.md). [Generated plate](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/art-pass/rattlesnake/refs-l7-v1.png).

## Identifying features

1. Compact stout snake with recognisable narrow neck behind a broad wedge head.
2. Raised small scales above eyes, not horns or antlers on ordinary adults.
3. Sandy ground colour, dark dorsal blotches and keeled scales.
4. Short narrow terminal tail, sharply distinguished from the thick midbody.
5. Segmented terminal rattle, not a ball or single long cone.
6. Limbless coils at rest; lateral travelling contact arcs in sidewinding.
7. Neonate has a button, not an adult stack; large stacks are not a reliable
   biological age counter because shedding and breakage vary.

## Body plan

| Part | Adult / B (snout-to-vent centreline) | Level0 direction |
|---|---|---|
| Head | .05–.07 | relatively larger wedge, no cartoon jaw |
| Narrow neck region | .05–.08 | preserve narrowing |
| Legs | 0 | 0 |
| Tail before rattle | .06–.10 | short with one button |
| Maximum diameter | .045–.065 | smaller absolute bulk, same continuous plan |

The midbody carries most mass; the rattle must not become a second heavy head.
Sidewinding needs a travelling wave and lifted sections between ground contacts;
it is not ordinary walking, nor a python coil simply moved sideways. Legend's
large coils, horns and sand ripple are existing fantasy styling and not natural
adult anatomy targets here.

Real resting animal is compact with subtler raised eye scales and less
uniformly bumpy texture than generation. Generated rattle is conspicuous but
must not dictate relative size or segment count. Eight continuous whole bodies
fit; top has adult segmented rattles, bottom terminal buttons. Moving cells
show extension, but do NOT demonstrate the requested lifted sidewinding contact
arcs convincingly: use them for anatomy only, never as animation approval.

## Ranked graph handoff

1. **Body/contact:** shared continuous snake body plus sidewinding contact-wave
   support, distinct from the python's resting pose.
2. **Rattle:** terminal hierarchical articulated segments/button, age-selectable
   and attached to a fine tail, not body-scale decorative beads.
3. **Head:** wedge skull, narrow neck and modest supraocular scales; keeled
   skin is secondary to silhouette and must not become stone armour.

**MISSING GRAPH FEATURES:** sidewinding contact-wave pose, segmented terminal
rattle/button family, supraocular scale control. Generic serpent prior/reptile
head/markings are present; no legs or external ears needed.

## Legend concepts — proposed pick A, Ed to judge

[Three concepts, both headings](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/art-pass/rattlesnake/legends-l7-v1.png).
Cactus flats story: pale gravelly sand, saguaros/Joshua trees, prickly pear and
dry tussocks (`art/areas/cactus-flats/recipe.js` text).

| Concept | Animal nature and age | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| A: epoch bell | Exceptionally long worn hollow rattle emits tail-centred sound ripples | **Proposed pick:** warning sound becomes ancient power, not an added ornament |
| B: sandglass | Translucent amber rattle contains sand | Fits desert, but glass decoration weakens the recognisable keratin rattle |
| C: path guardian | Great coils, old brow scales and terminal copper offerings | Strong age, less distinctive magic; jewellery unnecessary beside A |

Checklist: (1) sandy sidewinder coils; (2) broad wedge head/narrow neck;
(3) small supraocular horns, not antlers; (4) short tail into enlarged hollow
rattle; (5) ripples originate at rattle, never at a head halo; (6) broken/worn
segment edges and healed scars. Signature: **resonant keratin rattle**.
Retain body ratios; increase coil diameter through mass1.2× and extend rattle
display up to.25B. Segment count is fantasy design, never an age measurement.

Additional graph needs: articulated segment chain, hollow/worn material details,
tail-local effect anchor and safe motion envelope avoiding self-intersection.
Sound visualisation may recur for bat or toad, but this one is a mechanical
tail instrument/warning, not flight sonar or a vocal sac. Images cannot prove
actual sound/gait. Current source legend has rattleBig/ripple/horns
(`rattlesnake/genome.js`); rendered current comparison requested6708034797.
