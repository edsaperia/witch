# L9 flight / ground addendum

Authority: full [updated1866#6707952715](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/pull/1866#issuecomment-6707952715), 7 November 2026. Reference targets only.

## Pose sheets and actual inspection
Each eight-panel adult sheet requests standing folded, walking folded, downstroke, upstroke, intermediate, held glide, takeoff, landing (left-to-right, top then bottom). Images are separate pose proposals, not a sequentially validated flap cycle.
- [Griffin](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/art-pass/griffin/flight-v1.png): clearly folded ground wings and separate foretalons/hind paws; flight torso is level and head forward. Fanned primaries show in upstroke/glide. Some spread tips touch panel borders and hindlegs trail rather than tuck; do not copy those contacts/cropping.
- [Feathered serpent](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/art-pass/featheredserpent/flight-v1.png): ground coil/fold differs from extended flying body; no legs. Wing root/ruff separation improves, but wing span fills panel borders and takeoff/landing contacts are only suggested. Crest is too subdued compared with source plumage.
- [Sphinx](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/art-pass/sphinx/flight-v1.png): folded ground wings, four paws, level forward flight and spread primary fans visible. Generated wings are much larger than the small adult target; treat as winged legend-scale anatomy proposal, not approval to enlarge adult wings. Upper upstroke tips are close to panel border.

## Photo cross-check
Visually inspected [Golden Eagle Gliding](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Golden_Eagle_Gliding.jpg): layered inner/outer wing feathers, hooked beak, extended fore-aft flight body. Tip crop limits span measurement. This supports feather structure/glide posture only, not flap timing, six-limb fantasy anatomy or takeoff/landing.
A distant NPS perched eagle and a zoo head portrait were downloaded and inspected, but were inadequate for folded-wing/contact checks. A third [full-body perched golden eagle](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Golden_eagle_perched_on_rock_-_DPLA_-_adcd79c37d7ba3294209854bf402b46d.jpg) (Warren Garst, Colorado State University Libraries/DPLA, CC BY-SA4.0) shows both folded wings close to the flanks, layered feathers hanging beside the trunk, and toes gripping rock. This supports the folded-ground silhouette and talon contact target, not hidden elbow angles. It differs from the first ground sheets' loosely raised wings. The lion walking and python comparison photos linked in species notes support only grounded borrowed anatomy. No real photo is republished.

## MISSING GRAPH FEATURES
1. Torso flight pitch distinct from ground posture; head-forward neck pose and independently tucked/trailing legs.
2. Feather upper-arm/elbow/wrist articulation and fanned primaries, separate from already existing wing socket labels.
3. Enough distinct flap phases to read at game size; held-wing glide/soar.
4. Folded ground/perched wing state; contact-aware takeoff/landing transitions for grounded-until-attack creatures.
5. Age-specific wing absence/nubs, without adding flight to wingless young.

Frosttroll, hydra and unicorn have no wings. Frostwyrm is explicitly wingless in its audited genome but the coordinator note names it under membrane types. Coordinator question on PR1905 must settle whether that means a new variant; no wing addition or invented flight reference until resolved. A winged dragon/wyrm variant would need finger-ray membrane construction, not feather fans.

All generated prompts, tool/date and synthetic status are in each species' provenance.json beside its images. Camera is approximate 29.8deg down and34.4deg from side; no exact projection, animation or runtime proof.
