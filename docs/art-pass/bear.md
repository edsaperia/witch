## Identifying checklist

1. Highest muscular mass over the shoulders, not rump.
2. Back descends smoothly to a lower rounded rump.
3. Low hanging rounded belly without canine tuck.
4. Head forward/down at or below hump; broad skull, continuous tapered muzzle.
5. Small round lateral adult ears and small eyes with catchlight.
6. Thick distal forearms, broad clawed paws; heel-down hind feet.
7. Barely visible tail; cub rounder, bigger-headed, shorter-muzzled and shorter-legged.

## Body plan

Adult: head 0.25–0.35B; visible neck 0.10–0.20B; shoulder-to-ground 0.55–0.70B; tail 0.04–0.08B. Cub: head 0.33–0.42B, support height 0.40–0.55B, shorter muzzle. B is shoulder-to-rump torso length, not nose-to-tail length.

Shoulder/chest carry the upper silhouette, ventral mass hangs between limbs, lower rounded haunch. Avoid isolated ball shoulders or an equally tall rear hump.

All ratios use torso B from shoulder to rump excluding head, neck and tail; limb-chain length and standing support height are different quantities. Ranges are authored starting targets, not biological measurements inferred from generated pictures. Tune against real anatomy after a graph render.
