## Identifying features

1. Long low, broad trunk, without a mammal's pinched waist or high shoulders.
2. Long flattened tapering jaws; nose and eyes sit high on the head.
3. Four short outward-bent legs; broad splayed feet, not cat paws.
4. Heavy tail root, long laterally compressed tail and dorsal tail keels.
5. Bony dorsal scute rows, distinct from generic pebbly scale texture.
6. Almost no externally readable neck; no external ears.
7. Juvenile slim, cross-banded and long-tailed; not a round-headed plush adult.
