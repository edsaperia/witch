## Recognition checklist

- Broad fan ears over shoulders
- Flexible tapering trunk below mouth
- Paired upper-jaw tusks outside trunk
- Tall pillarlike legs
- Round load-bearing pads and short nails
- Long tufted tail
- Calf has shorter trunk and no visible tusks

## Proportions and graph

Adult modelling ranges use shoulder-to-rump length as 1. In neutral stance: shoulder joint to ground 0.88–1.11, skull back to nose 0.32–0.44, shoulder to poll along the neck 0.10–0.19, tail root to tip 0.38–0.55. These are approximate art targets inferred from silhouettes, not measured biological ranges. Head length excludes horns, antlers, tusks and trunk.

Priority order: Articulate trunk → Fold broad ear fans → Keep pad contact. Graph gap at #1866 revision `0a884af6`: Independent flexible trunk with two terminal fingers, curved upper tusks, hinged ear fans and padded feet with nail arcs.
