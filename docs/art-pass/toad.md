# Toad — skeleton target

Target: common-toad-type terrestrial body, not a long-legged smooth frog.
Adult and postmetamorphic toadlet level0; both headings, resting/crawling.
[Method](l7-method.md). [Generated plate](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/art-pass/toad/refs-l7-v1.png).

## Identifying features

1. Squat broad pear-shaped body with heavy folded hindquarters.
2. Broad blunt head, with little externally distinct neck.
3. Prominent oval parotoid glands immediately behind the eyes.
4. Granular/warty skin in earthy tones, not a glossy smooth frog suit.
5. Short sturdy forelimbs, longer folded hindlimbs, spread clawless digits.
6. High-set bronze eyes with horizontal pupils.
7. No tail after metamorphosis; toadlet has the same terrestrial plan, smaller
   and less bulky, not an aquatic tadpole or an adult with oversized eyes.

## Body plan

| Part | Adult / B | Level0 direction |
|---|---|---|
| Head | .25–.35 | relatively larger, still blunt and broad |
| Neck | 0–.03 externally | retain broad connection |
| Foreleg chain | .40–.55 | fine but weight-bearing |
| Hindleg chain | .80–1.15, folded | less bulky thighs, retain folds |
| Tail | 0 | 0 for postmetamorphic game stage |
| Body width / depth | .60–.80 / .40–.55 | slimmer toadlet, no inflated balloon belly |

Bulk lies in abdomen and paired thighs; short forearms brace the front. Folded
leg chain length is not standing leg height. Keep the body low during a slow
crawl. This reference does not authorise changing the game's gait to hopping.
Parotoid glands should read as a pair of skin masses, not ears or a crown.

## Ranked graph handoff

1. **Folded hindlimbs:** independently proportioned fore/hind chains and deep
   folded thigh posture, unlike four equal mammal legs.
2. **Head:** broad low skull plus paired parotoid masses and high-set eyes.
3. **Skin/age:** irregular shallow warts and slimmer toadlet, avoiding a scaled
   adult with giant eyes or hard crystalline nodules.

**MISSING GRAPH FEATURES:** reviewed folded anuran hindlimb/contact family,
parotoid gland and wart-distribution controls; accurate four/five digit feet.
Regional tissue, webbed-foot vocabulary and head sockets exist but do not prove
these. No tail, ears, horns or wings in the natural target.

## Legend concepts — proposed pick A, Ed to judge

[Three concepts, both headings](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/art-pass/toad/legends-l7-v1.png).
Wetland story: wet mud, puddles, reeds/rushes and willows (`art/areas.js` text).

| Concept | Animal nature and age | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| A: deep-song elder | Great translucent throat sac, ancient folds and calm dark eyes | **Proposed pick:** a marsh voice made powerful, distinct from a frog-prince crown |
| B: rain gatherer | Warts hold small shining water drops | Fits wetland and skin, but shares surface-water idea with many species |
| C: reed-tongue prophet | Long curling tongue with a lit tip and old ankle offering | Strong nature cue, but tongue flourish risks reading as a weapon rather than age |

Checklist: (1) heavy squat tailless body; (2) broad head/parotoids;
(3) folded hindquarters and all four limbs; (4) large throat sac hangs below,
not replacing head; (5) old scars/uneven warts; (6) light confined to vocal
membrane. Signature: **deep-song throat bladder**. Adult ratios retained,
trunk width1.2×; inflated sac diameter.35–.50B, leaving head and forefeet readable.
Common toad does not naturally have this giant external balloon: it is an
explicit legend fantasy, consistent with the game's existing `legend:[sac]`,
not a claim about Bufo anatomy.

Additional graph needs: inflatable membrane rooted beneath jaw with stored
deflated/inflated shape, bounded luminous interior and soft throat folds.
Generic membrane socket is present but not proof of inflation/contact control.
Any sound-ring repeat elsewhere needs a reason: toad is vocal resonance,
rattlesnake mechanical warning. Current rendered comparison requested6708034797;
