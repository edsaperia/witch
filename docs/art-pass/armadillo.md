## Identifying checklist

1. Low domed carapace, not fur-covered round mammal.
2. Rigid shoulder and pelvic shields separated by flexible middle bands.
3. Long narrow tapered snout close to ground.
4. Upright narrow ears.
5. Short strong legs and digging claws.
6. Long ring-armored gradually tapering tail.
7. Sparse hairy belly; young compact, relatively bigger head/ears.

## Body plan

Adult: head+snout 0.30–0.45B; exposed neck 0.05–0.12B; support height 0.20–0.35B; tail 0.65–0.95B; ear 0.12–0.20B. Juvenile head 0.38–0.48B and shorter tail. Carapace width 0.45–0.60B. Approximate design intervals only.

Shell makes the upper dome; soft ventral body and legs remain below it. Heavy central trunk, small forward probe head. Keep shields distinct from flexible band section.

All ratios use torso B from shoulder to rump excluding head, neck and tail; limb-chain length and standing support height are different quantities. Ranges are authored starting targets, not biological measurements inferred from generated pictures. Tune against real anatomy after a graph render.

## Shared graph requirements

Graph has shell sockets, not a demonstrated articulated banded carapace. Needs paired rigid shields plus deforming/overlapping transverse middle bands, ringed tapered tail and digging claws. Legend’s rolling glowing ball is a fantasy pose/state; nine-banded anatomy does not justify claiming real full-ball closure.
