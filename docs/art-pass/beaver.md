## Identifying checklist

1. Broad low pear-shaped body, heavier rump.
2. Blunt broad head merging into short thick neck.
3. Very small round ears and small lateral eyes.
4. Wide horizontally flattened scaly paddle tail.
5. Small dextrous forepaws, much larger webbed hind feet.
6. Low four-foot terrestrial stance, dragging/trailing tail.
7. Brown dense fur/whiskers; kit rounder and bigger-headed, not miniature otter.

## Body plan

Adult: head 0.25–0.33B; visible neck 0.05–0.12B; support height 0.20–0.35B; tail 0.45–0.65B long, 0.22–0.35B wide and very shallow; hind foot 0.20–0.30B. Kit head 0.32–0.42B; shorter tail/limbs but preserve broad paddle identity.

Rump and belly dominate; low chest, neck almost lost in coat. Tail is an appendage, not a second rump mass. Upright feeding can exist separately, not default walking.

All ratios use torso B from shoulder to rump excluding head, neck and tail; limb-chain length and standing support height are different quantities. Ranges are authored starting targets, not biological measurements inferred from generated pictures. Tune against real anatomy after a graph render.
