## Identifying checklist

1. Very long black-tipped ears, not round rabbit ears.
2. Long narrow head, large lateral eyes.
3. Large raised haunches over folded hind legs.
4. Much longer hind feet than forefeet.
5. Slim front limbs under narrower chest.
6. Arched torso and short dark-top pale-under tail.
7. Tawny agouti coat; leveret fully furred/open-eyed, shorter ears and bigger relative head.

## Body plan

Adult: head 0.25–0.33B; neck 0.10–0.20B; fore support height 0.45–0.65B; hind limb chain 0.85–1.10B unfolded; hind foot 0.30–0.45B; tail 0.10–0.16B; ear 0.30–0.45B. Leveret head 0.32–0.42B, shorter ears/limbs.

Rear-biased mass, powerful thighs and narrow chest; hock/fold supplies height change. Do not turn haunch into an armor dome or equal-legged dog.

All ratios use torso B from shoulder to rump excluding head, neck and tail; limb-chain length and standing support height are different quantities. Ranges are authored starting targets, not biological measurements inferred from generated pictures. Tune against real anatomy after a graph render.
