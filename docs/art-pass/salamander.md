# Salamander — skeleton target

Target: fire salamander anatomy, robust and low; black/yellow warning pattern.
Adult and postmetamorphic juvenile level0, both headings, resting/walking.
[Method](l7-method.md). [Generated plate](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/art-pass/salamander/refs-l7-v1.png).

## Identifying features

1. Broad low rounded head, large but not cartoonish dark eyes.
2. Little visible neck; head flows into a thick cylindrical trunk.
3. Four short sideways-bent legs with fine clawless digits.
4. Rounded fleshy tapering tail, without a newt's blade/crest.
5. Moist black skin with irregular yellow warning patches, not scale armour.
6. Subtle costal grooves and glandular bulges behind eyes, not external ears.
7. Juvenile is smaller/slimmer but already limbed and patterned; no aquatic
   external gills for the game's terrestrial level0.

## Body plan

| Part | Adult / B | Level0 direction |
|---|---|---|
| Head | .22–.28 | modestly larger share, keep flattened skull |
| Neck | .03–.07 | almost absent externally |
| Leg chain | .28–.40 | finer, not mammal paws |
| Tail | .60–.85 | retain rounded taper, not tiny stump |
| Trunk width / depth | .24–.34 / .18–.26 | less heavy abdomen |

Bulk sits through abdomen and broad head, evenly low, without a hump. Tail root
continues the trunk, then tapers; feet project laterally, with small digits.
In both headings distinguish a salamander from a scaled lizard and from a
tailless squat toad. Yellow is pigment on skin, not raised chunks of material.

## Ranked graph handoff

1. **Continuous low body:** broad head, short neck and rounded tail, independent
   of the more delicate newt's fin/crest.
2. **Sprawl/feet:** lateral limbs, four foredigits/five hind, no claws; gait
   must support a low trunk without a mammalian high stance.
3. **Surface/age:** skin grooves and irregular warning patches, juvenile
   proportions distinct from merely reducing adult scale.

**MISSING GRAPH FEATURES:** amphibian digit/sprawl family and shallow gland/costal
groove controls. Existing regional tissue/markings and tail chain should be
reused. No dorsal crest, wings or external ears for the natural target.

(3) four clawless limbs; (4) round tapering living tail with healed transition;
(5) old scars/weathered pigment contrast with soft new tissue; (6) glow restricted
to regenerated region. Signature: **living tail renewal**. Keep adult ratios,
increase old trunk bulk1.15× and tail length up to1.1× adult target. It is not a
shedding event; do not draw a cast-off tail, bleeding stump or broken bones.

through a tail chain, healed scars and bounded translucent/emissive material.
