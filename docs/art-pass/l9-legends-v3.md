# L9 legend third pass — readable silhouettes and pixel screening

This reference-only pass preserves the v1/v2 material and records the coordinator's simplified brief. Each species has two proposed silhouettes (A/B), a one-line pick rationale, and body-plan notes. The intended test is a whole ancient animal readable at 64–128 px beside a ~50 px witch: one or two exaggerated existing features, restrained armour/decorations/scars, and only small magic accents. Body-carried scenes are explicitly excluded.

## Species decisions

| Species | Concept A | Concept B | Pick and why it reads | Body-plan notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| featheredserpent | Huge feather ruff, paired feather fans, tiny turquoise tips | Long feather tail streamers, bronze collar | A: ruff + limbless coil survives the small silhouette test | limbless coil, feather-fan scale curve, collar/ring socket, scar decals |
| frosttroll | Stone-heavy forearms, small frost glow | Oversized white beard, broad hands, tiny ice runes | B: beard and shoulders are a single unmistakable mass | shoulder/arm mass, beard volume, plate sockets, scar decals |
| frostwyrm | Icicle beard and long tail | Swept horns and dorsal ice ridge | B: horns + ridge remain readable without wings or scenery | wingless neck/tail, horn curve, ridge sockets, weathering decals |
| griffin | Oversized feather wings and taloned forelegs, one spark | Lion haunches, crest and long tail, faint storm glow | A: wing span and talons identify the animal at a glance | wing sockets, foreleg/talon proportions, crest scale, barding sockets |
| hydra | Seven-head fan with long necks and scars | Broad continuous coils with varied seven heads | A: head fan counts cleanly at small size | strictly limbless, seven neck/head sockets, ring sockets, scar decals |
| sphinx | Massive lion haunches, nemes, tiny eye glow | Broad wings, tuft tail, one star accent | A: haunch + nemes make the sphinx read before details | lion body, wing sockets, nemes/band sockets, face weathering |
| unicorn | Heavy horse, long neck, chipped oversized horn | Huge mane/hindquarters, tiny healing droplets | A: horn + long neck are the fastest silhouette read | horn length curve, mane volume, barding sockets, scar decals |
