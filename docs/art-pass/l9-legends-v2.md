## Fit, variety and cross-lane spread
Available current summaries read for L2 (bib/season/sentinel/digging/quills/sleep/acorns/fog sail), L5 (claw/fleece/ears/incisors/armour/seed lights), L6 (listening disc/hoard/throat reflection/deep song), L7 (tide/scent/slough/rattle/crest/renewal/song/strata), L8 (jaws/claw/eyes/eyespots/brood/star shell/hairs/hearth). L1 summary and L4 signatures cited in L8's spread review were also consulted. Their new second-pass picks are incomplete; this is not a global uniqueness claim. Coordinator checks final64-species spread.

Justified repeats:
- Rock/world weathering serves different stories: troll literally becomes trollwood boulders; sphinx embodies its buried sandstone city; griffin has secondary cliff-like haunches because it belongs to a high rocky eyrie. Do not give them identical rock armour.
- Star-like light repeats in glacier ridges, lapis nemes and L8 snail's star map. Wyrm imprisons ancient light in ice, sphinx watches the sky over buried epochs, snail maps a spiral. Different materials/anatomy; retain for fit, subject to coordinator spread review.
- Hydra spectral shed histories overlap L7 python's slough. Hydra foreground remains seven living heads and regenerated history; reduce full-body shed-skin similarity if canonical selects both. No extra living heads or ring of eyes.
- Water is unicorn's healing moonpool/mane, troll's frozen beard falls, and hydra's drowned habitat. It has different anatomical carriers and is not a shared costume.
- Offerings/shrines are secondary signs of reverence, not the same jewellery on every species.
