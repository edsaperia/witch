# Legend batch: the elders of their kinds (ART-GUIDE §0c)

Ten legends redrawn as the elder of their kind, from Golf's ★ picks in `docs/art-review/mocks/legend-audit.md`. Each one keeps the animal's own nature and adds age, power and one piece of magic. There are no generic crystals, flames, wings or trees on the back.

The sheets show each species twice: the current version above (baby, young, adult and legend, with the witch for scale) and the new legend below. Only the legend changes; babies, young and adults draw as before.

- [`legend-batch-1.png`](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/archive/art-review/mocks/legend-batch/legend-batch-1.png): wolf, boar, stag, elk, bear.
- [`legend-batch-2.png`](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/archive/art-review/mocks/legend-batch/legend-batch-2.png): lynx, otter, beaver, toad, owl.

| Species | Current legend | New legend |
|---|---|---|
| Wolf | Crystal spines and a belly mane | A shorter mane, a pale ruff and scars; its howl rises in rings of moonlight |
| Boar | Glowing wisps over its back | Its old tusks and back kept, the wisps gone; scars, and the earth furrowed up ahead of its snout |
| Stag | A crystal antler | Antlers almost twice as big with extra tines, a small flame at every tip |
| Elk | Trees on its back | Vast palmate antlers, each palm a raised dish holding a glowing pool |
| Bear | Trees on its back | A towering hump and forequarters, glowing claws and scars |
| Lynx | Crystals | Ear tufts grown into long plumes, glowing at the tips |
| Otter | Crystals | Floating on its back in a ring of ripples, a stone and a pearl on its belly |
| Beaver | Crystals | Great incisors and a gnawed log in its jaws |
| Toad | A crown | A huge glowing throat sac that swells as it breathes |
| Owl | A ring of eyes | An enormous facial disc, rimmed and ringed, with far bigger eyes |

Once Ed approves, the build PR also redraws each new legend's sleeping form. It is checked by `node art/check.mjs`.
