## The creature

A new bird builder, `penguin`, on the bird template:
- upright, with a dark back and a white front;
- flippers held out at its sides;
- a small round head and a long pointed beak;
- short orange feet.

Its two walk frames rock it from foot to foot, which is the waddle. The belly-slide is for the build: a movement in which the game lays this sprite down and slides it.

Its ages:
- **Baby:** a downy grey chick with a dark cap.
- **Young:** grey-brown, with a white front.
- **Adult:** black and white, with an orange beak stripe and orange patches at its neck.
- **Legend** (revised after Ed's note: exaggerate what makes it unusual, not a generic crown): a towering emperor, its flippers flung wide like oars, a blazing orange gorget down its breast, a longer beak, and its downy chick standing on its feet before it, as emperors carry theirs.

**Size against the witch:**
- The chick is about a third of her height.
- The adult stands about 1.4 times her height, like an emperor at the witch's scale (big, but not one of the big beasts).
- The legend is about 3.5 times her height.

## The area: Penguin rookery

A cold, rocky shore. It is kept apart from the arctic fox's tundra and the polar bear's pack ice: those areas aren't on claude/prototype yet, so I couldn't compare them side by side.
- **Ground:** snow over dark wet shingle, the rock showing through in patches.
- **Walls:** black sea rocks.
- **Small:** pebbles and tussock grass.
- **Trees** (Ed's rule): wind-stunted pines and birches crouched low (krummholz), leaning with the wind, in sparse stands.
- **Set piece:** the rookery's ring of pebble nests (the generated stone ring, low stones round a trodden clearing).
