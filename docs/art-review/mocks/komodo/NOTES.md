# Komodo dragon: mock (unknown-04, the rocky island east; 2026-10-12)

Ed: more animals for later islands, each with its own area type. The Komodo dragon takes unknown-04's place (rocky, phase 3, bearing 89). This is the mock, for approval before the build. Its code is on branch `claude/komodo-mock`, not for merging.

[`komodo-mock.png`](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/archive/art-review/mocks/komodo/komodo-mock.png) is the whole sheet; [`lineup.png`](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/archive/art-review/mocks/komodo/lineup.png) and [`area.png`](https://github.com/edsaperia/witch/blob/claude/ed-shots/previews/archive/art-review/mocks/komodo/area.png) are its two rows at full size.

## The creature

A four-legged genome record (on the existing quadruped builder):
- a long, low, heavy body on sprawled legs;
- a long, flat, blunt head;
- a new `monitor` tail: thick at its root, as long again as the body, dragged low and curling round.

Its ages:
- **Baby:** banded yellow and green, as real hatchlings are (they live up trees).
- **Young:** speckled.
- **Adult:** heavy and grey-brown.
- **Legend:** a dragon in earnest, with a crest of spines down its back and long hooked claws.

Its texture is scales.

**Size against the witch:**
- The baby is about her height but long.
- The adult stands about 1.8 times her height, inside the bigger species' 1.45 to 2.1. It is about three times her length, so it reads as big through its length while staying low.
- The legend is several times her size, as legends are.

## The area: Dragon scrub

A dry, rocky savannah. Following Ed's rule ("trees are good, we should try and put them into areas"), it has trees suited to it:
- **Ground:** pale dry savannah grass over ochre dust (the tall-grass ground in dry colours).
- **Walls:** rock outcrops and boulders.
- **Small:** thorn scrub and dry grass tufts.
- **Big:** flat-topped acacias (a new tree genome: a thin forked trunk under a wide, layered, flat crown), with fan palms among them (the date palm genome as Komodo's lontar palm).
- **Set piece:** a great sun-warmed basking rock (the generated stone heap with a slab on top, bare of moss).
- **Layout:** scattered, open, few trees, wide glades.
