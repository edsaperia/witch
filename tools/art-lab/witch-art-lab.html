Witch Art Lab

Night in the forest: twilight everywhere, and the witch's glow shows the true colours near her. Pick the variant you like best and it becomes the new style; the next six grow from it. Drag knobs to steer by hand.

Pick one

Map

A whole run's forest: 20 × 20 areas cut by Boris the Brave's fractal jittered Voronoi. Each colour is an area type, never beside itself; white dots are area centres, where the clearings are; the ringed area in the middle holds the dancefloor. "New forest layout" draws a new map; "Border detail" sets how many fractal layers shape the borders.

Bestiary

Thirty forest animals, each as legend, adult, young and baby, in the current style under even light, turned towards you (or away, for creatures walking up the screen). Legends grow the magical features listed.

Sigils

Each creature's sigil: its name written in the forest's magic, in its own neon. Leashed creatures' sigils float above the witch, newest at the bottom; placing one writes it on the ground as the leashing rune, and picking it up lifts it back. The stack sways gently while she is still and trails behind her when she flies.

Below, each sigil at the four creature levels (baby, young, adult, legend), flat and then on the ground at the game's angle, drawing on.

Sprite sheet

Set pieces

Every area's set piece, its landmark, five to a row with the witch for scale. The twenty new ones are built in 3D at landmark size; the first ten are drawn smaller (the prototype scales those up).

Relics

The modern world, reclaimed by the forest: cars, trolleys, cones, broken highway and odds and ends; the overgrown playground's pieces; the sports grounds' pieces (tennis, baseball, football, a basketball hoop). Then each arrangement as suggested, the witch at its middle.

Decorations

World decorations any area can have, placed sparsely as discoveries: twelve ruins (weathered, then overgrown), rocks, freak trees, and the lake kit (water, shore band, reeds, lily pads), with the witch for scale.

Paths

A path kind laid along a curve with a branch meeting it, as the game would sweep it; then its strip, end, Y and T junction textures; then the 3D pieces (edge props, stairs, bridges, railway landmarks), with the witch for scale.

Trees

An area's trees across their whole range of heights, from saplings to the occasional giant that pokes above the canopy, with the witch for scale, and how the area lays out its vegetation (its layout, which the prototype's generator reads).

Witch

The witch in every pose, turned towards you (top) and away (bottom): three frames of her hover, the fast-flight lean, flying flat out over the treetops (three frames), braking hard (two frames), then rising to the treetops and descending to the ground, two frames each. Below, on foot (towards, then away), after her hover for scale: standing (three idle frames), landing and taking off (three each), talking (four gestures), placing and lifting a sigil (three each), and sitting on her terrace (two).

Home

The witch's treehouse, a camper van crossed with a castle and stuck in a giant tree, with her sitting on its terrace as the game starts: turned towards you, its base alone (as seen from the ground, under the canopy), and turned away.

Soundsystems

What the party defends: a sound-system stack hewn from magic stone, with glowing crystal cones. Each of the three stacks is shown playing (three frames of the cones pumping), damaged (two frames of flicker) and destroyed, with the witch for scale. Below them, the dancefloor speakers: one column each, twelve round the dancefloor, the far half facing in and the near half out so each shows us its front, drawn at three angles (15°, 45° and 75° from facing us; mirrored for the other side), playing, damaged and destroyed.
