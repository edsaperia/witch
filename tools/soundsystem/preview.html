Witch Soundsystems

Every area's soundsystem on a real map, as the game deals them: grown from its own genome, turned along the line to the dancefloor, and bigger the further out it stands. Reroll deals the same map a fresh set; no two in a set look alike.

This set

North is up, as the game's camera looks. Those north of the dancefloor face it; those south of it face away from it, so every one shows its front to the camera. None turns more than 60° from facing us, so a wide stack never stands side-on. Sizes run from 0.8 to 1.6 times the old stacks' height between 120 m and 1000 m out.
