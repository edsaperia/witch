Witch Music Studio

The game's own synth engine. Design a patch on the left and play it on the keyboard (or your computer keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K, Z and X for octaves). Drop a MIDI file from Logic on the right and every track plays on the patch you pick for it, through the game's mix. Copy for the builders gives your new and changed patches as text to paste in a message. Nothing here changes the game; your work is kept in this browser.
